## 🎯 语法焦点

**间接引语** — 间接引语：时态后退规则

## 📖 课文

"I will took three months tomorrow," Lin Wei said. → Lin Wei said that he/she would took three months the next day. "reported the issue is open-source." → Lin Wei said the reported the issue was open-source.

## 📐 本课核心

[复习第 199 课的规则](199-action-items.md)

**示范：** `He said that he was working on the fix.` — 他说他正在修复中。

**避免：** ❌ `He said that "I will go."` → ✅ `He said that he would go.`（间接引语不加引号，时态后退）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"用户需求"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `He said that "I will go."` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
