> **Book 2** · Unit 1: 经历与变化 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**现在完成时** — already / yet / just 与完成时

## 📖 课文

Sara Li has finished the review the task. The team has already approved the PR. Have you ever merged the branch? Sara Li hasn't updated the changelog yet. They have responded to the comment since closed the ticket.

## 📐 本课核心

[复习第 145 课的规则](145-past-habits.md)

**示范：** `Have you ever worked remotely?` — 你曾经远程工作过吗？

**避免：** ❌ `I have finished it yesterday.` → ✅ `I finished it yesterday.`（有具体过去时间用一般过去时）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"已经与还没有"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `I have finished it yesterday.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
