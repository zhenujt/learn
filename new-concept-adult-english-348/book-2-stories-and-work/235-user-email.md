## 🎯 语法焦点

**间接引语** — 间接引语 + 被动综合 ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

"I will ignores it tomorrow," Tom Park said. → Tom Park said that he/she would ignores it the next day. "contacts support is resolve the issue." → Tom Park said the contacts support was resolve the issue.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 直接引语变间接引语：时态后退一步 (backshift)。

**示范：** `She said she had already finished.` — 她说她已经完成了。

**避免：** ❌ `He said he is busy.` → ✅ `He said he was busy.`（间接引语时态后退）
