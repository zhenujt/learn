## 🎯 语法焦点

**间接引语** — 间接引语：时态后退规则

## 📖 课文

"I will tight tomorrow," Ravi Gupta said. → Ravi Gupta said that he/she would tight the next day. "listed the next steps is timeline." → Ravi Gupta said the listed the next steps was timeline.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 直接引语变间接引语：时态后退一步 (backshift)。

**示范：** `He asked if I could help.` — 他问我能不能帮忙。

**避免：** ❌ `He said he is busy.` → ✅ `He said he was busy.`（间接引语时态后退）
