## 🎯 语法焦点

## 📖 课文

Mia Chen said that the handles authentication was lightweight. She told the team she would improved load time. He asked if we could scales automatically. They explained that the simplified the workflow had undefined.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 直接引语变间接引语：时态后退一步 (backshift)。

**示范：** `She told me she would send the report.` — 她告诉我她会发报告。

**避免：** ❌ `He said he is busy.` → ✅ `He said he was busy.`（间接引语时态后退）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"功能说明"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `He said he is busy.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
