## 🎯 语法焦点

**定语从句：who / which / that** — 非限定性定语从句 ⟲：..., which ... 🔄

## 📖 课文

The developer who design consistency is Mia Chen. The framework, which is user research findings, works well. The task that accessibility improvements has been completed. Mia Chen is the person who feedback integration.

## 📐 本课核心

[复习第 193 课的规则](../book-2-stories-and-work/193-opening.md)

**示范：** `The framework that we chose is lightweight.` — 我们选的框架很轻量。

**避免：** ❌ `My laptop, that is new, works well.` → ✅ `My laptop, which is new, works well.`（非限定性从句不用 that）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"连接词"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `My laptop, that is new, works well.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
