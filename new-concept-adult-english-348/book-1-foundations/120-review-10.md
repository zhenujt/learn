## 🎯 语法焦点

**将来时：be going to / will** — 将来时 + 情态动词综合 ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

Lin Wei is going to follow up next by Friday. The team will confirm the plan after the review. Lin Wei won't miss the step until the tests pass. "Will you document the outcome?" "Yes, I will."

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** be going to + 动词原形：已计划好的将来行动。I am going to deploy tomorrow.

**示范：** `It is going to rain — take an umbrella.` — 要下雨了——带把伞。

**避免：** ❌ `I won't to come.` → ✅ `I won't come.`（won't 后直接加原形）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"第十单元复习"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `I won't to come.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
