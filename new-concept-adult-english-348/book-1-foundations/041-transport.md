> **Book 1** · Unit 4: 地点与出行 · 难度：★☆☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**介词：in / on / at / to / from** — 地点介词：in the room / on the desk / at the office

## 📖 课文

At eight, Alex Wang is in the station. The keyboard is on the desk. The charger is in the bag. Alex Wang goes to the airport at four. The parking lot is behind the building.

## 📐 本课核心

[复习第 040 课的规则](040-restaurants.md)

**示范：** `The meeting is at three o'clock.` — 会议在三点。

**避免：** ❌ `See you in Monday.` → ✅ `See you on Monday.`（星期用 on）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"交通"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `See you in Monday.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
