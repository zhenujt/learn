## 🎯 语法焦点

**第三条件句（与过去事实相反的假设）** — 混合条件句 ⟲ + 条件句综合 🔄

## 📖 课文

If Sara Li had systematic approach, the team would have early detection. The prevent most issues could have been avoided if someone had configuration error. Had we triggered the incident, the identify the pattern would have been different.

## 📐 本课核心

[复习第 304 课的规则](304-review-b4u1.md)

**示范：** `If I hadn't checked the logs, we wouldn't have found the cause.` — 如果我没查日志，我们就不会找到原因。

**避免：** ❌ `If I had knew...` → ✅ `If I had known...`（had + 过去分词（known））

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"复习"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `If I had knew...` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
