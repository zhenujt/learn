# G03 代词与指代：it、this、that、they、one

<!-- sentence-audio: fenced-only -->

> **频率：🔴 必学** ｜ 阶段：小学 → 初中 ｜ 会议数据：`that` 每万词约 141 次（含连词等用法），`this` 约 118 次，`it` 约 90 次，`one` 约 49 次，`they/them` 约 38 次，`he/she/him/her` 约 22 次，`something/anything/nothing/everything` 约 20 次
>
> [English version](03-pronouns-and-reference.md) · [上一课：G02 名词、冠词与数量表达](02-nouns-articles-quantifiers.zh.md) · [下一课：G04 be 动词与 There be](04-be-and-there-be.zh.md) · [语法目录](README.zh.md)

## 这一课解决什么问题

会议里大家不会每次都把名字说全，而是用 `it`、`this`、`that`、`they`、`this one` 代替。看这条字幕：

```text
I checked it with them yesterday, and they said this one is fine, but that one still needs a fix.
我昨天跟他们确认过了，他们说这个没问题，但那个还需要修。
```

每个词都认识，可是 `it` 是什么？`them` 是谁？`this one` 和 `that one` 又分别指哪个工单？如果追不上，这句话就等于没听懂。

把代词想象成代码里的**指针（引用）**：它本身没有内容，你必须找到它指向的那个东西。

**学完这一课，你应该能：**

1. 用对人称代词的五种形式（`I / me / my / mine / myself`），不再把 `he` 和 `she` 说混。
2. 分清 `it`、`this`、`that` 的区别，并认出 `that` 的三种身份。
3. 用 `one / ones` 避免重复名词，比如 `the next one`、`this one`。
4. 在字幕里快速判断 `it / this / they` 指的是什么。

## 1. 人称代词：一张表记住五种形式 🔴

| 主格（做主语） | 宾格（做宾语） | 形容词性物主 | 名词性物主 | 反身代词 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | me | my | mine | myself |
| you | you | your | yours | yourself / yourselves |
| he | him | his | his | himself |
| she | her | her | hers | herself |
| it | it | its | — | itself |
| we | us | our | ours | ourselves |
| they | them | their | theirs | themselves |

### 1.1 主格和宾格：看位置 🔴

- **主格**放在谓语动词前面（做主语）。
- **宾格**放在动词或介词后面（做宾语）。

```text
I will send it to you.
我会发给你。（I 主语；it 宾语；you 在介词 to 后面）

Can you help me with this?
你能帮我看一下这个吗？

Mia and I will work on it together.
我和 Mia 会一起做。（主语里要用 I，并且把自己放在后面）

Let's ask them.
我们问问他们吧。（Let's = Let us）
```

### 1.2 he 和 she：中国同学最容易说混 🔴

中文口语里"他"和"她"读音一样，所以很多人说英语时会随口混用。**说同事之前，先想一下性别再开口。** 如果不确定或不想指明性别，可以用 `they`：

```text
Ken is out today, so he will reply tomorrow.
Ken 今天不在，他明天回复。

Anna said she would review it.
Anna 说她会评审。

If anyone has questions, they can ping me.
如果有人有问题，可以私信我。（they 指不确定的某个人）
```

### 1.3 you、you guys、you all 🟡

`you` 既是"你"也是"你们"。为了说清楚是"你们大家"，会议里常说 `you guys` 或 `you all`：

```text
Can you guys see my screen?
你们能看到我的屏幕吗？

Thank you all for joining.
谢谢大家参加。
```

## 2. 物主代词：my 和 mine 🔴

**`my / your / our / their` 后面必须接名词；`mine / yours / ours / theirs` 单独使用。**

```text
That's all for my part.
我这部分就这些。

Nothing from my side.
我这边没有更新。

Is this PR yours or mine?
这个 PR 是你的还是我的？（🟡）

Their team is working on the same feature.
他们团队也在做同样的功能。
```

> ⚠️ `its` 是"它的"，`it's` 是 `it is / it has` 的缩写：`The page and its style`（这个页面和它的样式）；`It's done.`（做完了）。

## 3. 反身代词 🟢

会议里很少用（每万词约 1 次），能认出几个固定说法就够了：

```text
I can do it by myself.
我可以自己做。

They don't depend on each other.
它们之间互不依赖。

The page itself is fine, but the API is slow.
页面本身没问题，但接口很慢。
```

## 4. it：会议里最忙的代词 🔴

### 4.1 指前面说过的东西或整件事

```text
I found a bug in the login page, and I'm fixing it now.
我在登录页发现了一个 Bug，正在修。（it = the bug）

We discussed the timeline. It's still not final.
我们讨论了时间安排，还没定下来。（it = the timeline）

I'll look into it.
我去查一下。（it = 刚才说的那个问题）
```

### 4.2 形式主语：It's + 形容词 + to do / that … 🔴

英语不喜欢把很长的主语放在句首，就先用 `it` 占位，把真正的主语放到后面：

```text
It's hard to say right now.
现在还很难说。（真正的主语：to say right now）

It's good to know.
知道了，挺好。

It's not necessary to change the API.
没有必要改接口。

It looks like the build is broken.
看起来构建坏了。

It seems that nobody has checked it yet.
好像还没人看过。
```

这里的 `it` **不指任何东西**，翻译时不用译出来。

### 4.3 时间、情况、天气

```text
It's 9 PM here.
我这里现在晚上 9 点。

It's fine. Don't worry.
没关系，别担心。

It's been a busy week.
这周挺忙的。
```

### 4.4 常见固定说法

```text
That's it.
就这些了。

Go for it.
去做吧。

Let's leave it for now.
先放一放。

I'll make it.
我能赶上。
```

## 5. this / that / these / those 🔴

**核心区别：远近。** 但在会议里，"远近"往往是**时间和屏幕上的远近**：

| 词 | 会议里通常指 | 例子 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `this` | 正在共享的屏幕上的东西；我正要说的事 | `This is the PR I mentioned.` |
| `that` | 对方刚说的话；之前提到的事 | `That makes sense.` |
| `these` | 屏幕上的多个东西 | `These two tickets are related.` |
| `those` | 之前说过的多个东西 | `Those issues are fixed now.` |

```text
This is the new dashboard.
这是新的仪表盘。

That's a good point.
说得好。

That's right.
没错。

That makes sense to me.
我觉得有道理。

These are the open tickets for this sprint.
这些是这个 Sprint 未关闭的工单。

I think those comments are already resolved.
我觉得那些评论已经处理了。
```

**回应别人时几乎总是用 `that`：** `That sounds good.`、`That's fine.`、`That's all.` 这里的 `that` 指"你刚才说的内容"。

### 5.1 that 的三种身份 🔴

字幕里 `that` 出现得特别多，但它不一定是"那个"：

| 身份 | 怎么认 | 例子 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 指示代词"那个" | 单独做主语或宾语 | `That's fine.` |
| 连词（引导宾语从句，可省略） | 跟在 `think / know / said / make sure` 后面 | `I think that it works.`（见 [G18](18-noun-clauses.zh.md)） |
| 关系代词（引导定语从句） | 跟在名词后面 | `the bug that we found`（见 [G19](19-relative-clauses.zh.md)） |

```text
That is the main problem.
那就是主要问题。（代词）

Ken said that the fix is ready.
Ken 说修复已经好了。（连词，可以省略）

This is the ticket that I created yesterday.
这是我昨天建的工单。（关系代词，指 ticket）
```

## 6. one / ones：避免重复名词 🔴

`one` 代替前面说过的**可数名词**，复数用 `ones`。主持人逐个过工单时几乎一直在说：

```text
Let's move to the next one.
我们看下一个。（one = 下一个工单 / 议题）

Do we need to vote for this one?
这个需要投票吗？

Which one is better?
哪个更好？

The old tests are fine, but the new ones are failing.
旧的测试没问题，新的在失败。

Not this one, sorry. The one below.
不是这个，抱歉，是下面那个。
```

两个提醒：

- `one` 不能代替不可数名词：❌ `a better one feedback`。
- `one of the + 复数` 是"其中一个"，`one` 这里是数词：`One of the tests is failing.`

## 7. 不定代词：someone、anything、nothing… 🔴

| | -one / -body（人） | -thing（事物） |
| --- | --- | --- |
| some- | `someone`（某人） | `something`（某事） |
| any- | `anyone`（任何人，用于问句/否定） | `anything` |
| no- | `no one` / `nobody`（没人） | `nothing` |
| every- | `everyone`（大家） | `everything` |

**规则：不定代词做主语时，谓语用单数。**

```text
Does anyone have any questions?
有人有问题吗？

Everyone is here, so let's get started.
人都到齐了，我们开始吧。

Is there anything else?
还有别的吗？

Nothing from my side.
我这边没有。

Something is wrong with the pipeline.
流水线出了点问题。

Can someone take this ticket?
有人能接这个工单吗？
```

形容词要放在不定代词**后面**：`something new`、`anything else`、`nothing special`。

### 7.1 other、another、the other、others 🟡

| 说法 | 意思 | 例子 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `another` | 另一个（还有很多） | `Let's try another option.` |
| `the other` | 两个里的另一个 | `One is for iOS; the other is for Android.` |
| `other + 复数` | 其他的 | `other teams` |
| `others` | 其他人 / 其他东西 | `I'll check with others.` |
| `the others` | 剩下的全部 | `The others are done.` |
| `each other` | 互相 | `They depend on each other.` |

## 8. 在字幕里追踪指代

找 `it / this / they` 指什么，按顺序试这四条：

1. **找最近的、单复数一致的名词。** `it` 找单数，`they` 找复数或团队。
2. **共享屏幕时，`this` 和 `here` 多半指屏幕上的东西。**
3. **`it / this / that` 常常指"刚才那整件事"**，而不是某个具体的词。
4. **`they` 经常指"另一个团队、公司或某些人"**，不一定在这句里出现过。

> 字幕：We noticed that you have reached out to the tool's dev team, and they provided a reference doc.

`they` = `the tool's dev team`（最近的、表示"一群人"的名词）。

> 字幕：All of them are blocked by the approval. If you ask the cloud engineer to set up the resources, they will ask you for the approved document.

第一个 `them` = 前面说的那些任务；第二个 `they` = `the cloud engineer`（泛指"云平台的工程师们"，所以用 `they`）。

## 9. 真实会议例句（改编）

```text
Ken is taking it, but I don't think it's started yet.
这个由 Ken 负责，不过我觉得还没开始做。

These two tasks depend on this one.
这两个任务依赖于这一个。

I see that Anna approved it, but I can't edit it yet.
我看到 Anna 已经批准了，但我还不能编辑。

If anyone hasn't read it, they can read through the page.
如果有人还没看过，可以把这个页面读一遍。

Okay, let's have a second vote for this one.
好，这个我们再投一次票。

So that's something we need to figure out with the native team.
所以这是我们需要和 Native 团队一起搞清楚的事。

Except this one, does anyone have other topics to discuss?
除了这个，还有人有别的议题要讨论吗？
```

## 10. 字幕里怎么认

1. **`this this this` / `it, it's, it's not` 是口吃和重启，只看最后一次。**
2. **he 和 she 混用很常见。** 非母语同事经常说错，以名字为准。
3. **`you mean …?` 后面常接对方刚说的内容**，是在确认理解，见 [G05](05-questions-and-negatives.zh.md)。
4. **非母语说法能看懂即可：**

| 字幕里可能看到 | 标准说法 |
| --- | --- |
| `Ken … she will update it`（Ken 是男生） | `he will update it` |
| `Does anyone else has questions?` | `Does anyone else have questions?` |
| `in my side` | `on my side` / `from my side` |
| `I and Mia will do it.` | `Mia and I will do it.` |
| `Me also.` | `Me too.` |
| `This PR, it is ready.` | `This PR is ready.`（口语可以接受，写作不要） |

## 11. 常见错误

| ❌ 错误 | ✅ 正确 | 原因 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Ken said she will fix it.` | `Ken said he will fix it.` | 男生用 `he`，女生用 `she` |
| `Me and Mia will do it.` | `Mia and I will do it.` | 主语用主格，自己放后面 |
| `Send it to I.` | `Send it to me.` | 介词后用宾格 |
| `Does anyone has questions?` | `Does anyone have questions?` | `does` 后用原形 |
| `Everyone are here.` | `Everyone is here.` | 不定代词用单数 |
| `It's status is green.` | `Its status is green.` | `its` = 它的 |
| `Is very hard to say.` | `It's very hard to say.` | 需要形式主语 `it` |
| `I need else something.` | `I need something else.` | 修饰语放在不定代词后面 |
| `This is mine PR.` | `This is my PR.` / `This PR is mine.` | `mine` 不能放在名词前 |

## 12. 你可以直接用的句子

That makes sense. Thanks.

It's hard to say right now. I'll update you tomorrow.
现在还很难说，我明天告诉你。

Can someone take this one?
有人能接这个吗？

## 13. 练习

**A. 下面加粗的代词指什么？**

1. I created a ticket for the login bug, and Kevin will pick **it** up.
2. We asked the security team, and **they** said we need an approval.
3. There are two options. **The first one** is faster, but **the other** is cheaper.
4. Okay, this is the new dashboard. **These** are the charts for last week.
5. — We can skip the demo today. — **That**'s fine with me.

**B. 填入正确的代词**

1. Can you send the link to ___ (我)?
2. Lily is on leave, so ___ will reply next week.
3. ___ hard to say when it will be ready.
4. Does ___ (有人) have any questions?
5. The new tests pass, but the old ___ fail.
6. Emma and ___ (我) will do the demo.

**C. 改错**

1. Everyone have the access now.
2. Is not necessary to update the doc.
3. Ben is busy today, so she can't join.
4. Its already merged.

<details>
<summary>答案</summary>

**A.**
1. the ticket（登录 Bug 的工单）
2. the security team
3. the first one = 第一个方案；the other = 另一个方案
4. 屏幕上的那些图表（上周的图表）
5. 对方刚说的"今天跳过演示"这件事

**B.** 1 me　2 she　3 It's　4 anyone　5 ones　6 I

**C.**
1. Everyone has access now.
2. It's not necessary to update the doc.
3. Ben is busy today, so he can't join.
4. It's already merged.
