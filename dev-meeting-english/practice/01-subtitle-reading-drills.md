## How to Use These Drills

The 12 subtitle snippets below are adapted from real stand-ups and weekly sprint syncs. Names and product names are anonymized. To keep them realistic, fillers, repeats, non-native forms, and speech-recognition (ASR) errors are **kept on purpose**.

Work through each snippet in the same five steps:

1. **① Find the skeleton:** who + did / is doing / will do what. Delete fillers such as `okay`, `so`, `I mean` first; see [G23](../grammar/23-spoken-grammar.md).
2. **② Mark the grammar:** name 2–4 grammar points and the lesson that covers them.
3. **③ Rewrite it** in natural, correct English.
4. **④ Say what it means** in simple words.
5. **⑤ Learn one sentence to say yourself:** pick one sentence and adapt it to your own work.

Do it yourself first, then open "Answers". Your rewrite does not have to match exactly; it only has to be correct and keep the meaning.

**ASR tip:** an everyday word that makes no sense in the sentence (`face`, `conference`, `brands`, `relief`) is probably a misheard technical word.

## Drill 1: Opening a Stand-Up ★

> **Mia:** Hi, good morning, good afternoon, good evening, everyone.
> **Ken:** Hello, good evening.
> **Mia:** OK, let's get it started. So let's go from the phase one and, um, let me check.
> **Mia:** Okay, hi guys, is my screen visible?

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**① Skeleton:** `let's get started` / `let's start from phase one` / `is my screen visible?`

**② Grammar:**

- `let's` + base verb: a suggestion for the group → [G06](../grammar/06-imperatives-lets-please.md)
- `is my screen visible?`: a question with `be` → [G05](../grammar/05-questions-and-negatives.md)
- `the phase one`: no `the` before a noun + number; say `phase one` → [G02](../grammar/02-nouns-articles-quantifiers.md)
- `get it started`: common in speech; the standard phrase is `get started`

**③ Rewrite:**

```text
Okay, let's get started. Let's start with phase one.

Hi everyone, can you see my screen?
```

**④ Meaning:** The host starts the meeting with the first phase and checks that the screen share works.

**⑤ Say it yourself:**

```text
Okay, let's get started. Can everyone see my screen?
```

</details>

## Drill 2: Your Turn — No Update ★

> **Mia:** And Ken, do you have any update or?
> **Ken:** Oh no, nothing from my side.
> **Mia:** Okay. Anna?
> **Anna:** Yeah, just one. The login page is ready for review. Yeah, that's all from my side, and Leo, you can go ahead.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**① Skeleton:** `Do you have any updates?` / `(I have) nothing (to update)` / `The login page is ready for review.` / `Leo, you can go ahead.`

**② Grammar:**

- `do you have any update or?`: a `do` question; the final `or?` is short for `or not?` → [G05](../grammar/05-questions-and-negatives.md)
- `Nothing from my side.`, `Just one.`: fragments → [G23](../grammar/23-spoken-grammar.md)
- `ready for review`: `for` + purpose → [G16](../grammar/16-prepositions.md)
- `go ahead`: phrasal verb, "please continue / your turn" → [G14](../grammar/14-phrasal-verbs-and-collocations.md)

**③ Rewrite:**

```text
Ken, do you have any updates?

No, nothing from my side.

Just one: the login page is ready for review. That's all from me. Leo, go ahead.
```

**④ Meaning:** Ken has no update. Anna's login page can be reviewed, and she passes the turn to Leo.

**⑤ Say it yourself:**

```text
Just one update: the settings page is ready for review. That's all from me.
```

</details>

## Drill 3: A One-Line Update (with an ASR Error) ★☆

> **Ken:** Yesterday, and thanks for Leo's help, we already merged this to our main brands to unblock others.
> **Ken:** So, yeah, the, the build is green now.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**① Skeleton:** `we merged this to our main branch` / `the build is green now`

**② Grammar:**

- `brands` is an ASR error for `branch` → [G23](../grammar/23-spoken-grammar.md)
- `Yesterday … we already merged`: with `yesterday`, use the past simple; `already` is unnecessary → [G08](../grammar/08-past-simple-and-present-perfect.md)
- `to unblock others`: infinitive of purpose → [G13](../grammar/13-to-do-doing-done.md)
- `thanks for Leo's help` inside a sentence should be `thanks to Leo's help`

**③ Rewrite:**

```text
Thanks to Leo's help, we merged this into our main branch yesterday to unblock others.

The build is green now.
```

**④ Meaning:** With Leo's help, the change was merged yesterday so other people are no longer blocked, and the build passes.

**⑤ Say it yourself:**

```text
I merged the fix into the main branch yesterday, so the build is green now.
```

</details>

## Drill 4: gonna and Screen Sharing ★☆

> **Emma:** Uh, we're gonna be remapping the, the touch libraries as a, as a first step.
> **Emma:** Okay, okay, I'm gonna share my screen. So can you see my screen?
> **Ken:** Yes, we can see it.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**① Skeleton:** `we're going to remap the touch libraries` / `I'm going to share my screen` / `can you see my screen?`

**② Grammar:**

- `gonna` = `going to`: a planned future → [G09](../grammar/09-future-will-going-to.md)
- `going to be remapping`: future continuous, "will be in the middle of doing" → [G10](../grammar/10-other-tenses.md)
- repeated `the, the`, `as a, as a`: merge them → [G23](../grammar/23-spoken-grammar.md)
- `can you see…?`: a modal question → [G11](../grammar/11-modal-verbs.md)

**③ Rewrite:**

```text
As a first step, we're going to remap the touch libraries.

Okay, I'm going to share my screen. Can you see it?
```

**④ Meaning:** The first planned step is remapping the touch libraries; then she shares her screen.

**⑤ Say it yourself:**

```text
As a first step, we're going to update the API contract.
```

</details>

## Drill 5: A Designer's Spoken Update (British Style) ★★

> **Emma:** The only update I have, we've started the Sprint 1 components and we are also looking at remapping the icon library as well. On the design side, Lily is going to be taking care of that. So just as an FYI to you guys for the native pod.
> **Emma:** And Leo is also going to share, not today, don't worry, Leo, I'm not putting you on the spot, but share the kind of token structure and strategy there as well for us all to kind of discuss and get aligned on. So maybe not today, but maybe sometime this week or next would be really cool.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**① Skeleton:** `We've started the Sprint 1 components.` / `Lily is going to take care of the icon library.` / `Leo is going to share the token structure and strategy (this week or next).`

**② Grammar:**

- `we've started`: present perfect, "already started" → [G08](../grammar/08-past-simple-and-present-perfect.md)
- `we are looking at remapping`: present continuous + `-ing` after a preposition → [G07](../grammar/07-present-simple-and-continuous.md), [G13](../grammar/13-to-do-doing-done.md)
- the aside `not today, don't worry, Leo, I'm not putting you on the spot`: she interrupts herself and returns with `but share…` → [G23](../grammar/23-spoken-grammar.md)
- `for us all to discuss`: `for + person + to do` → [G13](../grammar/13-to-do-doing-done.md)
- `kind of` twice: vagueness; delete it
- `put someone on the spot`: idiom, "put someone under pressure in public"

**③ Rewrite:**

```text
My only update is that we've started the Sprint 1 components. We're also looking at remapping the icon library.

Lily will take care of that on the design side. This is just an FYI for the native pod.

Leo will also share the token structure and strategy, so we can all discuss it and get aligned. Not today, but maybe this week or next.
```

**④ Meaning:** Design work for Sprint 1 has started; Lily owns the icon library work; Leo will present the token plan later, not today.

**⑤ Say it yourself:**

```text
Just an FYI: Lily will take care of the icon updates on the design side.
```

</details>

## Drill 6: Confirming with right? ★★

> **Ken:** Okay, so this one is like the self-hosted one, which will not require any like software purchasing. We can just use that like in the self-hosted AWS EC2 instance, right?
> **Priya:** Yes, yes, exactly.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**① Skeleton:** `This one is the self-hosted one.` / `We can use it in a self-hosted AWS EC2 instance, right?`

**② Grammar:**

- three `like`s: thinking pauses; delete them → [G23](../grammar/23-spoken-grammar.md)
- `which will not require…`: non-defining relative clause → [G19](../grammar/19-relative-clauses.md)
- `We can just use…`: `just` softens → [G11](../grammar/11-modal-verbs.md), [G23](../grammar/23-spoken-grammar.md)
- final `right?`: tag question asking for confirmation → [G23](../grammar/23-spoken-grammar.md)

**③ Rewrite:**

```text
So this is the self-hosted option, which doesn't require buying any software.

We can just run it on a self-hosted AWS EC2 instance, right?
```

**④ Meaning:** Ken checks his understanding: this option needs no software purchase and can run on their own EC2 instance. Priya confirms.

**⑤ Say it yourself:**

```text
So we can reuse the existing runner, right?
```

</details>

## Drill 7: Host Transitions (regarding to + ASR) ★★

> **Mia:** Okay, so if no question or comment regarding to the Sprint 1 scope, I will give an update for the status.
> **Mia:** …
> **Mia:** Okay, that's all for the web part. OK, I think we can move to the next face.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**① Skeleton:** `If (there are) no questions, I will give a status update.` / `We can move to the next phase.`

**② Grammar:**

- `if no question or comment`: `there are` is dropped; first conditional → [G21](../grammar/21-conditionals.md)
- `regarding to`: non-native; use `regarding` or `about` → [G16](../grammar/16-prepositions.md)
- `give an update for the status`: unnatural; say `give a status update`
- `face` is an ASR error for `phase` → [G23](../grammar/23-spoken-grammar.md)
- `That's all for the web part.`: `for` marks scope / closing → [G16](../grammar/16-prepositions.md)

**③ Rewrite:**

```text
Okay, if there are no questions or comments about the Sprint 1 scope, I'll give a status update.

Okay, that's all for the web part. I think we can move on to the next phase.
```

**④ Meaning:** The host checks for questions, gives the status update, then closes the web part and moves on.

**⑤ Say it yourself:**

```text
If there are no more questions, let's move on to the next topic.
```

</details>

## Drill 8: Reporting Someone's Conclusion ★★

> **Tom:** Yes, I checked with Ken today and he said that for this analysis currently, there is no further action for the component level, Node.js version bump or even SSR code change at this point. Yeah.
> **Mia:** Okay, got it. Thanks, Tom.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**① Skeleton:** `I checked with Ken.` / `He said (that) there is no further action.`

**② Grammar:**

- `he said that … there is…`: reported speech; still true, so no backshift → [G22](../grammar/22-reported-speech.md)
- `there is no further action`: there-be pattern → [G04](../grammar/04-be-and-there-be.md)
- `for this analysis`: `for` as a topic marker → [G16](../grammar/16-prepositions.md)
- `currently` and `at this point` repeat each other; keep one
- `Got it.`: fragment → [G23](../grammar/23-spoken-grammar.md)

**③ Rewrite:**

```text
I checked with Ken today. He said that, based on this analysis, no further action is needed at this point.

That means no component-level changes, no Node.js version bump, and no SSR code changes.
```

**④ Meaning:** According to Ken, nothing needs to change right now.

**⑤ Say it yourself:**

```text
I checked with Ken, and he said it's not a blocker at this point.
```

</details>

## Drill 9: Thinking Aloud (Restarts and I mean) ★★☆

> **David:** I do have one question regarding the website. I mean, I'm not really sure if we have finalized, like, how we gonna approach for the website. I mean, we have done the comparison, but have we concluded anything?
> **Mia:** Not yet. We, we are still waiting for the, the feedback from the design team.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**① Skeleton:** `I have one question.` / `I'm not sure if we have decided how we're going to approach the website.` / `Have we concluded anything?` / `We are still waiting for feedback.`

**② Grammar:**

- `I do have`: emphatic `do` → [G24](../grammar/24-advanced-grammar-overview.md)
- `I'm not sure if…`: `if` = "whether" in a noun clause → [G18](../grammar/18-noun-clauses.md)
- `how we gonna approach for`: `are` is missing; `approach` takes a direct object → [G23](../grammar/23-spoken-grammar.md)
- `we have done` / `have we concluded`: present perfect → [G08](../grammar/08-past-simple-and-present-perfect.md)
- `Not yet.`: fragment; `waiting for`: collocation → [G16](../grammar/16-prepositions.md)

**③ Rewrite:**

```text
I have one question about the website. Have we decided how we're going to approach it?

We've done the comparison, but have we reached a conclusion?

Not yet. We're still waiting for feedback from the design team.
```

**④ Meaning:** David asks whether the website approach has been decided. Mia says no decision yet; they are waiting for design feedback.

**⑤ Say it yourself:**

```text
I'm not sure if we've decided how to handle the old API. Have we reached a conclusion?
```

</details>

## Drill 10: Platform Update (Several ASR Errors) ★★☆

> **Nina:** For Sigma token sync, Lily is updating the POC conference page according to the comments we got last week, and we can have more detailed updates when Lily is back from POC tomorrow.
> **Nina:** For the Sigma plugin, according to your feedback yesterday, so we added a new backlog which to support the auto Teams notification for the submitter when a ticket is created. So, and we also planned it into a current space, current sprint.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**① Skeleton:** `Lily is updating the POC page.` / `We will have more updates when she is back.` / `We added a new backlog item.` / `We also planned it into the current sprint.`

**② Grammar:**

- ASR errors: `Sigma → Figma`, `conference → Confluence`, `back from POC → back from PTO` (back from leave), `current space → current sprint` (the speaker corrects herself) → [G23](../grammar/23-spoken-grammar.md)
- `For Figma token sync, …`: topic marker → [G16](../grammar/16-prepositions.md)
- `the comments we got last week`: relative clause without `that` → [G19](../grammar/19-relative-clauses.md)
- `when Lily is back tomorrow`: present tense for the future in a time clause → [G20](../grammar/20-adverbial-clauses.md)
- `according to …, so we added`: the `so` is extra; `a new backlog which to support` should be `a new backlog item to support`
- `when a ticket is created`: passive → [G12](../grammar/12-passive-voice.md)

**③ Rewrite:**

```text
For the Figma token sync, Lily is updating the POC page in Confluence based on last week's comments.

We'll have more details when she's back from PTO tomorrow.

For the Figma plugin, based on your feedback yesterday, we added a new backlog item to send an automatic Teams notification to the submitter when a ticket is created.

We've also added it to the current sprint.
```

**④ Meaning:** Lily is revising the POC page and will update the team after her leave. A new item for automatic notifications was added to this sprint.

**⑤ Say it yourself:**

```text
Based on your feedback, we added a new backlog item and put it into the current sprint.
```

</details>

## Drill 11: Long Sprint-Sync Paragraph — Story by Story ★★★

> **Mia:** Next one is a provide browser enable Harness container for web test suite, and currently we already completed two subtests under the story based on the solution last week we checked with Leo. We use the browser-enabled solution instead of image, and to continue, our developer will focus on other subtasks on this story for Linux and Windows operation system to build up cross-browser CI test lanes.
> **Mia:** And the visual test and E2E test are all completed in this brand. And the next one is a backward compatibility for the framework and Browder version support. I saw Ken just shared the document with all engineers in the channel and also requested Leo's review for the conference documents.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**① Skeleton:**

- `We have completed two subtasks.`
- `We use the browser-enabled solution.`
- `Our developers will focus on the other subtasks.`
- `The visual tests and E2E tests are completed.`
- `Ken shared the document and requested Leo's review.`

**② Grammar:**

- The first sentence **reads the story title aloud**: `provide browser-enabled Harness container for web test suite`, so the grammar looks strange. Treat the title as one noun.
- ASR errors: `subtests → subtasks`, `this brand → this sprint`, `Browder → browser`, `conference → Confluence` → [G23](../grammar/23-spoken-grammar.md)
- `currently we already completed`: use the present perfect `we have completed` → [G08](../grammar/08-past-simple-and-present-perfect.md)
- `the solution last week we checked with Leo`: word order; should be `the solution (that) we checked with Leo last week` → [G19](../grammar/19-relative-clauses.md)
- `to build up … test lanes`: infinitive of purpose → [G13](../grammar/13-to-do-doing-done.md)
- `are all completed`: passive / completed state → [G12](../grammar/12-passive-voice.md)
- `I saw Ken just shared…`: reporting → [G22](../grammar/22-reported-speech.md)
- `operation system → operating system`; `instead of image → instead of an image` → [G02](../grammar/02-nouns-articles-quantifiers.md)

**③ Rewrite:**

```text
The next story is about providing a browser-enabled Harness container for the web test suite.

We've completed two subtasks, based on the solution we agreed with Leo last week.

We're using the browser-enabled solution instead of a custom image.

Next, our developers will focus on the remaining subtasks to set up cross-browser CI test lanes for Linux and Windows.

The visual tests and E2E tests are all completed in this sprint.

The next one is backward compatibility for framework and browser versions.

Ken has shared the document with all engineers in the channel and asked Leo to review the Confluence pages.
```

**④ Meaning:** Two subtasks of the container story are done; cross-browser CI lanes are next. Visual and E2E tests are finished. The compatibility document is waiting for Leo's review.

**⑤ Say it yourself:**

```text
We've completed two subtasks, and next we'll focus on the remaining ones.
```

</details>

## Drill 12: Discussion — Release Before or After the Holiday? ★★★

> **Mia:** And another request for the release, we informed the customer that it will be released in the early of October. It's still in development. So if this ticket could be ready for test tomorrow, and the no issue found, that we can release it as well in tomorrow release. Otherwise, we need to consider to push the release to when we back from the holiday.
> **Ben:** Okay, sorry, which team is asking for the release?
> **Mia:** Let me check.
> **Ben:** Yeah, I think it's better to do the release after the holiday, just in case if they have any follow-up questions, we can answer them when we are available. If we do pre, like before the holiday, probably they will reset the questions while team members are taking the time off, and we are not able to get back to them.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**① Skeleton:**

- `We informed the customer that it will be released in early October.`
- `If the ticket is ready and no issues are found, we can release it tomorrow.`
- `Otherwise, we need to push the release.`
- `It's better to release after the holiday.`
- `If we release before the holiday, they may raise questions, and we won't be able to answer.`

**② Grammar:**

- `informed the customer that it will be released`: reporting + passive → [G22](../grammar/22-reported-speech.md), [G12](../grammar/12-passive-voice.md)
- `in the early of October` → `in early October`
- `if this ticket could be ready … and the no issue found, that we can…`: a broken conditional; should be `if this ticket is ready … and no issues are found, we can…` → [G21](../grammar/21-conditionals.md)
- `consider to push` → `consider pushing`: `consider` takes `-ing` → [G13](../grammar/13-to-do-doing-done.md)
- `when we back` → `when we're back`: missing `be` → [G04](../grammar/04-be-and-there-be.md)
- `Otherwise`: linking word → [G17](../grammar/17-conjunctions-and-linking.md)
- `it's better to do`: comparative + infinitive → [G15](../grammar/15-adjectives-adverbs-comparison.md)
- `just in case if` → `just in case`; `If we do pre, like before` is a restart; `reset` is an ASR error for `raise` → [G23](../grammar/23-spoken-grammar.md)
- `while team members are taking time off`: `while` clause → [G20](../grammar/20-adverbial-clauses.md)

**③ Rewrite:**

```text
For the other release request, we told the customer it would be released in early October, but it's still in development.

If the ticket is ready for testing tomorrow and no issues are found, we can include it in tomorrow's release.

Otherwise, we'll need to push the release to after the holiday.

I think it's better to release after the holiday, just in case they have follow-up questions.

If we release before the holiday, they might raise questions while the team is off, and we won't be able to get back to them.
```

**④ Meaning:** Mia explains the release options. Ben prefers releasing after the holiday so the team can answer customer questions.

**⑤ Say it yourself:**

```text
I think it's better to release it next week, just in case we find any issues.
```

</details>

## After the Drills

- **You found the skeleton in 9 or more snippets:** you can follow most meeting subtitles. Go on to [P02 Checkpoint Tests](02-checkpoint-tests.md).
- **Fillers and repeats keep stopping you:** reread Sections 1, 2, and 4 of [G23 Spoken Grammar](../grammar/23-spoken-grammar.md).
- **You find the skeleton but not the structure:** reread [G01 Sentence Skeleton](../grammar/01-sentence-skeleton.md) and [G18 Noun Clauses](../grammar/18-noun-clauses.md).
- **You often miss ASR errors:** make a list of your project's technical words and keep it next to you in meetings.

**Extra practice:** after each meeting, pick one subtitle passage you didn't understand and work through the five steps.
