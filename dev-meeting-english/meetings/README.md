# Meeting Situations: Using Grammar in Stand-Ups and Weekly Meetings

> [中文版](README.zh.md) · [Course home](../README.md) · [Grammar index](../grammar/README.md) · [Study Plan](../00-study-plan.md)

Grammar lessons teach you to **understand sentences**; this section teaches **what to say and when**. Each lesson covers the meeting moment, a fixed speaking formula, sentences grouped by function, a full dialogue adapted from real meetings (anonymized), signals that it is your turn, a personal fill-in template, and practice with answers.

## Seven Situations

| Lesson | When | First sentence to learn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [M01 Stand-Up Updates](01-stand-up-updates.md) | Your turn in the daily stand-up | `Nothing from my side.` / `I'm working on …` |
| [M02 Weekly Sprint Sync](02-weekly-sprint-sync.md) | The host walks through the board module by module | `For the web part, most stories are completed.` |
| [M03 Hosting and Screen Sharing](03-hosting-and-screen-sharing.md) | Opening, sharing a screen, switching topics | `Can you see my screen?` / `Let's move to …` |
| [M04 Asking and Clarifying](04-questions-and-clarifying.md) | You missed something or need to confirm | `Just to clarify, do you mean …?` |
| [M05 Opinions and Suggestions](05-opinions-and-suggestions.md) | Agreeing, disagreeing politely, proposing | `I think that works.` / `How about …?` |
| [M06 Blockers, Risks, and Help](06-blockers-risks-and-help.md) | You are stuck, late, or need help | `I'm blocked by …` / `Could you help me with …?` |
| [M07 Wrap-Up and Action Items](07-wrap-up-and-action-items.md) | Closing, owners, follow-up | `That's all for today.` / `I'll follow up on …` |
