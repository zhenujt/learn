# M03 主持会议、共享屏幕与切换话题

<!-- sentence-audio: fenced-only -->

> **频率：🔴 必学** ｜ 场景：开场、共享屏幕、点名、切换议题、Demo ｜ 会议数据：`any questions / comments / topics` 每万词约 16 次，`go ahead` 约 5 次，`let's move / let's go` 约 5 次，`let me share my screen` 约 4 次，`can you see my screen` + `is my screen visible` 约 4 次
>

## 这一课解决什么问题

每次轮到别人讲，第一句几乎都是：

```text
Let me share my screen. Can you see my screen?
我共享一下屏幕。大家能看到我的屏幕吗？
```

这一类"流程句"在会议里出现得非常多，但很少有人专门学。结果是：别人问 `Is my screen visible?` 你不知道该不该回答；轮到你做 Demo，你不知道怎么开口；主持人说 `Let's take it offline.` 你还在继续讲。

这些句子数量不多、非常固定，**背熟以后能让你在会上显得很从容**。总有一天你也要主持会议或者做 Demo，这一课一起准备好。

**学完这一课，你应该能：**

1. 用固定的句子开场、说明议程、点名、交接。
2. 共享屏幕并确认大家能看到；处理看不到屏幕、听不到声音这些小故障。
3. 邀请提问、切换话题、在时间不够时把讨论放到会后。
4. 听懂主持人的流程信号，知道会议进行到了哪一步。

## 1. 会议现场：主持人在做什么

主持人（host，常常是 Scrum Master）一场会要做这几件事：

| 步骤 | 主持人在做什么 | 典型句子 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 开场 | 打招呼、等人 | `Hi, good morning, good evening, everyone.` `Let's wait one more minute.` |
| 说明安排 | 告诉大家今天有哪几部分 | `Today we have three parts.` |
| 共享屏幕 | 打开看板或文档 | `Let me share my screen. Is my screen visible?` |
| 点名、交接 | 请某人讲 | `Ken, you can go ahead.` |
| 邀请提问 | 每段结束问一次 | `Any questions or comments?` |
| 切换 | 进入下一段 | `If no questions, let's move on.` |
| 控制时间 | 讨论太长时打断 | `Let's take this offline.` |
| 收尾 | 结束会议（见 M07） | `That's all for today. Thank you.` |

你们的会议是跨时区的（中国、印度、英国、美国），所以开场常常是一串问候：`Good morning, good afternoon, good evening, everyone.` 这不是口误，是**照顾每个时区的人**。

## 2. 固定公式

**主持一段会议的六步：**

> ① 开场 `Hi, everyone. Let's get started.` → ② 说安排 `Today we have …` → ③ 共享并确认 `Let me share my screen. Can you see it?` → ④ 讲或点名 `For …, …` / `Ken, you can go ahead.` → ⑤ 问问题 `Any questions or comments?` → ⑥ 切换 `If no questions, let's move on to …`

**轮到你做 Demo 或讲一段：**

> `Let me share my screen.` → `Can you see my screen?` → `Today I will show you …` → 演示 → `That's it for the demo. Any questions?` → `Let me stop sharing.`

## 3. 按功能分组的句子

### 3.1 开场、等人

```text
Hi, good morning, good afternoon, good evening, everyone.
大家早上好、下午好、晚上好。

Let's wait one more minute for others to join.
我们再等一分钟，等其他人进来。

I think most people are here, so let's get started.
人差不多到齐了，我们开始吧。

I see Ken hasn't joined yet, so let's start with the native part first.
Ken 还没进来，那我们先从 Native 部分开始。
```

### 3.2 说明今天的安排

```text
Today we have three parts.
今天有三个部分。

First, the high-level updates, then the story updates, and at the end, the post-scrum discussion.
先是整体消息，然后是 Story 进度，最后是会后讨论。

We have two demos today. Priya, you can do the first one.
今天有两个 Demo。Priya，你先来第一个。

Does anyone have any topic to bring up today?
今天有人有话题要提吗？
```

### 3.3 共享屏幕 ⭐ 最高频

```text
Let me share my screen.
我共享一下屏幕。

Can you see my screen?
大家能看到我的屏幕吗？

Is my screen visible now?
现在能看到我的屏幕吗？

Yes, I can see your screen.
能，我看到你的屏幕了。

Not yet. It's still loading.
还没有，还在加载。

I think you are sharing the wrong window.
你好像共享错窗口了。

Could you zoom in a little? The text is too small.
能放大一点吗？字太小了。

Okay, let me stop sharing. Ken, you can share yours.
好，我停止共享。Ken，你来共享吧。
```

别人问 `Can you see my screen?` 时，**一定要有人回答**。如果已经有人说了 `Yes`，你就不用重复。如果大家都不说话，你就说 `Yes, we can see it.`，这是一个很好的"开口练习"。

### 3.4 声音和网络问题 🟡

```text
Can you hear me?
能听到我说话吗？

Sorry, I was on mute.
不好意思，我刚才静音了。

I think you're on mute.
你好像静音了。

Sorry, you're breaking up a little. Could you say that again?
不好意思，你的声音有点断断续续，能再说一遍吗？

Sorry, my network is not stable today.
不好意思，我今天网络不太稳定。
```

### 3.5 点名与交接

```text
Ken, you can go ahead.
Ken，你开始吧。

Priya, do you want to start?
Priya，你要先开始吗？

Okay, over to you, Leo.
好，交给你了，Leo。

Nina, I saw you raised your hand. Please go ahead.
Nina，我看到你举手了，请说。
```

`go ahead` 在会议里有三个意思：①"你说吧"（请对方开始）②"你继续"（对方被打断后）③"去做吧"（同意对方的提议）。看上下文判断。

### 3.6 邀请提问

```text
Any questions or comments?
大家有什么问题或意见吗？

Does anyone have any questions regarding this part?
关于这部分，大家有什么问题吗？

If you have any questions during the demo, feel free to interrupt me.
演示过程中有问题，可以随时打断我。

You can also leave your questions in the chat.
也可以把问题发在聊天框里。
```

### 3.7 切换话题

```text
If no questions, let's move on to the next topic.
如果没有问题，我们进入下一个议题。

Okay, let's move to the platform part.
好，我们接着看平台部分。

That's all for the scrum updates. Let's move to the post-scrum discussion.
Scrum 更新就到这里，我们进入会后讨论。
```

### 3.8 控制时间、把讨论放到会后

```text
We are running out of time, so let's take this offline.
时间不太够了，这个我们会后单独聊。

Let's not go too deep into the details in this meeting.
这次会上我们先不深入细节。

Let's put this in the parking lot and come back to it later.
这个先记下来，晚点再回来讨论。

We have five minutes left.
我们还剩五分钟。
```

`take it offline` 不是"断网"，而是"**会后私下讨论**"。`parking lot` 是"暂存区"：把偏题的事记下来，之后再处理。

### 3.9 做 Demo

```text
Today I will do a quick demo of the token sync workflow.
今天我简单演示一下 Token 同步的流程。

As you can see here, the button now supports dark mode.
大家可以看到，这个按钮现在支持暗色模式了。

Let me go back to the previous page.
我回到上一页。

That's it for the demo. Any questions?
演示就到这里。有问题吗？
```

## 4. 完整对话：Alex 第一次主持会后讨论（改编自真实会议）

场景：Scrum 更新结束后，Grace 请 Alex 主持会后讨论。今天有一个 Demo。

```text
Grace: That's all for the scrum updates. Alex, can you host the post-scrum session today?
Grace：Scrum 更新就到这里。Alex，今天的会后讨论你来主持？

Alex: Sure. So, does anyone have any topic to bring up today?
Alex：好的。今天有人有话题要提吗？

Priya: Yes, I can do a quick demo of the PR automation.
Priya：有，我可以简单演示一下 PR 自动化。

Alex: Great. Priya, please go ahead.
Alex：好的。Priya，请开始。

Priya: Thanks. Let me share my screen. Is my screen visible?
Priya：谢谢。我共享一下屏幕。能看到我的屏幕吗？

Alex: Not yet. I can only see a black screen.
Alex：还没有，我只看到黑屏。

Priya: Sorry, let me share it again. How about now?
Priya：不好意思，我重新共享一下。现在呢？

Alex: Yes, now we can see it.
Alex：可以了，现在能看到了。

Priya: Okay. As you can see here, when someone requests a review on GitHub, a message is sent to our Teams channel automatically.
Priya：好。大家可以看到，有人在 GitHub 上请求评审时，消息会自动发到我们的 Teams 频道。

Ken: Sorry to interrupt. Does it also work for draft PRs?
Ken：不好意思打断一下。草稿 PR 也可以吗？

Priya: Not yet. That's a good point. I will check it later.
Priya：现在还不行。这个问题提得好，我之后看一下。

Priya: That's it for the demo. Any questions?
Priya：演示就到这里。有问题吗？

Ken: Looks good. But I think we need to discuss the permission part in more detail.
Ken：挺好的。不过权限那部分我觉得要再详细讨论一下。

Alex: Since we are running out of time, let's take the permission part offline. Ken and Priya, can you set up a quick call?
Alex：时间不太够了，权限这部分我们会后单独聊。Ken 和 Priya，你们可以约个短会吗？

Ken: Sure.
Ken：可以。

Alex: Thanks, Priya, for the demo. Any other topics?
Alex：谢谢 Priya 的演示。还有其他话题吗？

Alex: Okay, if no other topics, that's all for today. Thank you, everyone.
Alex：好，如果没有其他话题，今天就到这里。谢谢大家。
```

## 5. 听到这些说明……

| 听到 | 说明 | 你该怎么做 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Is my screen visible?` / `Can you see my screen?` | 讲的人在确认共享 | 没人回答时，说 `Yes, we can see it.` |
| `Let's get started.` | 会议正式开始 | 关掉其他窗口，打开字幕 |
| `Any questions or comments?` | 一段结束，可以提问 | 有问题这时问；没有就保持安静 |
| `Please go ahead.` / `Over to you.` | 轮到被点名的人说 | 如果是你，马上开始 |
| `Feel free to interrupt.` | 讲的人欢迎随时打断 | 可以用 `Sorry to interrupt, …` 提问 |
| `Let's move on.` / `Let's move to …` | 进入下一段 | 看是不是轮到你的模块 |
| `Let's take this offline.` | 这个话题会后再聊 | 停止讨论，会后找人 |
| `I have a hard stop at 8.` | 他 8 点必须离开 | 如果有问题要问他，尽快问 |

## 6. 字幕里怎么认

| 字幕里可能看到 | 实际意思 / 标准说法 |
| --- | --- |
| `is my ice cream visible` | `is my screen visible` |
| `I'm my screen baseball` | `is my screen visible` |
| `could you see my screen right now` | `can you see my screen now`（意思一样） |
| `post-scram session` | `post-scrum session` |
| `if no, let's move on` | `if not, let's move on` / `if there are no questions, let's move on` |
| `let's discuss about it offline` | `let's discuss it offline` |

**读法：** 流程句短、固定，**看到开头几个词就能猜出整句**。`Let me sh…` 一定是 share my screen；`Any que…` 一定是 any questions。把注意力留给后面的内容句。

## 7. 常见错误

| ❌ 错误 | ✅ 正确 | 原因 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `I share screen to you.` | `Let me share my screen.` | `share my screen` 是固定说法，不加 `to you` |
| `Everybody can see?` | `Can everyone see it?` | 疑问句要把 `can` 提前 |
| `Please open your mic.` | `Please unmute yourself.` | 英语说 `unmute`，不说 open the mic |
| `Open your camera.` | `Turn on your camera.` | 设备开关用 `turn on / turn off` |
| `Let's move to next topic.` | `Let's move to the next topic.` | `next topic` 前要有 `the` |
| `If no question, …` | `If there are no questions, …` | 完整句更清楚 |
| `Let's discuss about it later.` | `Let's discuss it later.` | `discuss` 后不加 `about` |

## 8. 你的主持模板

| 步骤 | 英文模板 | 中文提示 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 开场 | `Hi, everyone. I think most people are here, so let's get started.` | 打招呼、开始 |
| 安排 | `Today we have ________ parts. First, ________. Then, ________.` | 说议程 |
| 共享 | `Let me share my screen. Can you see it?` | 共享并确认 |
| 点名 | `________, you can go ahead.` | 请某人讲 |
| 提问 | `Any questions or comments on ________?` | 每段结束问一次 |
| 切换 | `If no questions, let's move on to ________.` | 进入下一段 |
| 控时 | `We are running out of time, so let's take ________ offline.` | 放到会后 |

做 Demo 时的版本：

```text
Let me share my screen. Can you see it? Today I will do a quick demo of the new date picker. As you can see here, you can now select a date range. That's it for the demo. Any questions? Okay, let me stop sharing.
我共享一下屏幕。能看到吗？今天我简单演示一下新的日期选择器。大家可以看到，现在可以选择一个日期范围了。演示就到这里。有问题吗？好，我停止共享。
```

## 10. 练习

**A. 选择最合适的回应**

1. 对方问：`Is my screen visible?`（你看到了）
2. 对方问：`Can you hear me?`（你只听到断断续续的声音）
3. 主持人说：`Alex, you can go ahead.`
4. 讨论越来越长，你是主持人，只剩 3 分钟。

a. `Sure. A quick update from my side.`　b. `Yes, we can see it.`　c. `We are running out of time, so let's take this offline.`　d. `Sorry, you're breaking up a little.`

**B. 改错**

1. Everybody can see my screen?
2. Let's move to next part.
3. Please open your mic, Ken.
4. Let's discuss about this offline.

**C. 用英文说**

1. 我们再等一分钟。
2. 我共享一下屏幕。现在能看到吗？
3. 演示过程中有问题，可以随时打断我。
4. 如果没有问题，我们进入平台部分。
