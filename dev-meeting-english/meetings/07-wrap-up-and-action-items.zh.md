# M07 收尾、确认分工与会后跟进

<!-- sentence-audio: fenced-only -->

> **频率：🔴 必学** ｜ 场景：话题收尾、会议结束、会后消息 ｜ 会议数据：`thank you / thanks` 每万词约 45 次，`bye` 约 21 次，`any (other) questions / topics` 约 16 次，`that's all` 约 11 次，`tomorrow` 约 6 次，`follow up` 约 4 次
>
> [English version](07-wrap-up-and-action-items.md) · [上一课：M06 阻塞、风险与求助](06-blockers-risks-and-help.zh.md) · [下一课：P01 字幕精读 12 练](../practice/01-subtitle-reading-drills.zh.md) · [会议目录](README.zh.md)

## 这一课解决什么问题

讨论了二十分钟，最后大家说 `Okay, sounds good. Bye.` 就下线了。第二天才发现：没人知道这件事到底谁来做。

会议的价值在于最后那一分钟：**结论是什么、谁来做、什么时候做完**。一句清楚的收尾是这样的：

```text
So, to summarize, Alex will update the ticket, and Priya will check the license by Friday.
总结一下：Alex 更新工单，Priya 在周五前确认许可证的事。
```

**学完这一课，你应该能：**

1. 用 `So, to summarize, …` 总结一个话题的结论。
2. 用「谁 + 做什么 + 什么时候」说出和确认行动项。
3. 主动认领任务，并说出会后跟进的承诺。
4. 用固定句子结束会议、跨时区道别，会后发一条简短的总结消息。

## 1. 会议现场：两种"收尾"

| 收尾类型 | 什么时候 | 典型句子 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 话题收尾 | 一个讨论结束时 | `So, we can take this as a follow-up item.` |
| 会议收尾 | 整场会结束时 | `Any other topics? … Okay, that's all for today.` |
| 会后跟进 | 会议结束以后 | `I'll update the ticket after the meeting.` |

在你们的真实会议里，会议收尾几乎每次都是同一个顺序：

1. 主持人问：`Any other topics?` / `Anyone have any other questions?`
2. 短暂沉默后：`It seems no, so that's all for today.`
3. 有人说：`We can end the call here.` / `We can drop the call then.`
4. 大家道谢、道别：`Thank you, everyone. Have a good day. Bye-bye.`

因为是跨时区会议，道别也照顾时区：对英国、美国同事说 `Have a good day.`，对中国、印度同事说 `Have a good night.` 或 `Good night.`。

**话题收尾最容易被忽略。** 你们的会议里 `action item` 每万词只出现不到 1 次，`follow up` 约 4 次，很多讨论最后只说 `we can discuss offline`。所以，**如果事情和你有关，你自己要确认清楚谁来做**。

## 2. 固定公式

**行动项（action item）= 谁 + 做什么 + 什么时候**

> `Alex` + `will update the ticket` + `by Friday.`

缺一样都不算清楚。少了"谁"，没人做；少了"什么时候"，就会一直拖。

**一个话题的收尾：**

> `So, to summarize, + 结论.` → `+ 谁 + will + 动作 + 时间.` → `Is that correct?` / `Does that work for everyone?`

**整场会的收尾（主持人）：**

> `Any other topics?` → `If not, that's all for today.` → `Thank you, everyone. Have a good day.`

## 3. 按功能分组的句子

### 3.1 总结一个话题

```text
So, to summarize, we will keep the local runner for now.
总结一下，我们暂时保留本地 Runner。

Okay, so the conclusion is that we go with the self-hosted website.
好，结论是我们用自建的网站。

So we agree to put this story on hold until the approval is done.
所以我们一致同意，审批完成前先暂停这个 Story。

Let me quickly recap the action items.
我快速回顾一下行动项。
```

### 3.2 确认谁来做 ⭐ 最重要

```text
Who will take this?
这个谁来做？

Alex, can you take this as a follow-up item?
Alex，这个你能作为后续事项跟进吗？

Sure, I'll take it.
好的，我来做。

Ken and Priya will follow up on this.
Ken 和 Priya 会跟进这件事。

I'll leave it to you to decide who picks it up.
谁来接，你们自己决定。

Let's create a follow-up story to track it.
我们建一个后续 Story 来跟踪。
```

被点名时，**直接接下**用 `Sure, I'll take it.`；如果做不了，说清原因：`I can take it, but not until next week, because I'm on the release this week.`

### 3.3 说清时间

```text
I'll update the ticket by end of day.
我今天下班前更新工单。

Can you share it before the next sync?
你能在下次同步会之前分享出来吗？

Let's check it again in tomorrow's daily sync.
明天的每日同步会上我们再看一下。

We will have a follow-up meeting next week.
我们下周再开一次跟进会。
```

`by Friday` 是"**最晚周五**"，`on Friday` 是"在周五那天"。行动项里几乎都用 `by`。详见 [G16](../grammar/16-prepositions.zh.md)。

### 3.4 放到会后讨论

```text
Let's discuss this offline.
这个我们会后单独讨论。

I'll set up a quick call with Ken tomorrow.
我明天和 Ken 约个短会。

Let's continue the discussion in the channel.
我们在频道里继续讨论。

I'll align with the design team offline and update everyone.
我会后和设计团队对齐，再同步给大家。
```

### 3.5 你会后要做的事（承诺）

```text
I'll send the link in the chat.
我把链接发到聊天框里。

I'll update the ticket after the meeting.
会后我更新工单。

I'll take a look and get back to you if I have any questions.
我看一下，有问题再找你。

I'll share the document in the engineering channel today.
我今天把文档发到工程频道。
```

`I'll …` 在这里是**承诺**。说出口就要做到。如果不确定，就说 `I'll try to …` 或给一个更晚的时间。

### 3.6 结束会议

```text
Any other topics?
还有其他话题吗？

It seems no, so that's all for today.
看来没有了，那今天就到这里。

Okay, we can end the call here.
好，我们就到这里结束吧。

Thanks for joining, and thank you for your time.
谢谢大家参加，感谢大家的时间。
```

### 3.7 跨时区道别

```text
Thank you, everyone. Have a good day.
谢谢大家，祝大家今天顺利。

Have a good night. Bye.
晚安，再见。

See you tomorrow.
明天见。

Have a nice weekend.
周末愉快。
```

### 3.8 会后消息（Teams 上写的总结）🟡

会后发一条简短的总结，是最有效的跟进方式。写的比说的容易，**适合英语还不够流利的你**。

```text
Hi all, here is a quick summary of today's discussion.
大家好，简单总结一下今天的讨论。

1. We will keep the local runner as a short-term solution.
1. 我们暂时保留本地 Runner 作为短期方案。

2. Alex and Priya will research the cloud runner option by Friday.
2. Alex 和 Priya 周五前调研云端 Runner 方案。

3. Ken will check the license status with the platform team.
3. Ken 和平台团队确认许可证状态。

Please let me know if I missed anything.
如果有遗漏，请告诉我。
```

## 4. 完整对话：会后讨论的收尾（改编自真实会议）

场景：会后讨论快结束了。大家刚讨论完"PR 评审通知自动化"。Grace 主持，Ken 是 Tech Lead，Priya 和 Alex 是开发。

```text
Grace: Okay, I think we have discussed a lot. So, to summarize, we want to send a Teams message automatically when someone requests a PR review.
Grace：好，我觉得我们讨论得差不多了。总结一下：有人请求 PR 评审时，我们想自动发一条 Teams 消息。

Ken: Yes. And we need to check if the automation needs a premium license.
Ken：对。另外我们得确认这个自动化需不需要高级许可证。

Grace: Right. So, who can take this?
Grace：对。那这个谁来做？

Alex: I can take the research part. I'll check the license and the existing documents.
Alex：调研的部分我来。我去确认许可证和现有的文档。

Grace: Great. When do you think you can share the result?
Grace：好的。你觉得什么时候能分享结果？

Alex: I think I can share it by Thursday, before the next sync.
Alex：我想周四之前可以，在下次同步会之前。

Ken: Sounds good. I have a document from the platform team about sending notifications to Teams. I'll share the link with you after the meeting.
Ken：好。我有一份平台团队写的关于往 Teams 发通知的文档，会后把链接发给你。

Alex: Thanks, Ken. That will help a lot.
Alex：谢谢 Ken，这会很有帮助。

Grace: Okay, so let me quickly recap. Alex will research the license and share the result by Thursday. Ken will send the document link today. Is that correct?
Grace：好，我快速回顾一下。Alex 调研许可证，周四前分享结果。Ken 今天把文档链接发过来。对吗？

Alex: Yes, that's correct.
Alex：对，没错。

Grace: Perfect. Any other topics?
Grace：好。还有其他话题吗？

Priya: Nothing from my side.
Priya：我这边没有。

Grace: Okay, it seems no, so that's all for today. Thank you, everyone.
Grace：好，看来没有了，那今天就到这里。谢谢大家。

Ken: Thanks, guys. Have a good day. Bye.
Ken：谢谢大家，祝你们今天顺利，再见。

Alex: Thank you. Have a good night. Bye-bye.
Alex：谢谢，晚安，拜拜。
```

注意 Alex 做对的三件事：**主动认领**（`I can take the research part.`）、**给出时间**（`by Thursday`）、**确认总结**（`Yes, that's correct.`）。

## 5. 听到这些说明……

| 听到 | 说明 | 你该怎么做 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `So, to summarize, …` / `Let me recap.` | 主持人在总结结论 | 认真读，这是最重要的信息 |
| `Who will take this?` / `Can someone pick this up?` | 在找负责人 | 想做就说 `I can take it.` |
| `Alex, can you take this as a follow-up item?` | 任务被分给你 | `Sure, I'll take it.` 并说时间 |
| `I'll leave it to you.` | 让你们自己决定 | 会后和同事商量好 |
| `Let's discuss offline.` / `Let's set up a call.` | 会后单独谈 | 主动约时间 |
| `Any other topics?` | 会议快结束了 | 有事现在说，没事保持安静 |
| `We can end the call here.` / `We can drop the call.` | 会议结束 | 道谢、道别 |
| `I'll get back to you.` | 对方会后回复你 | 记下来，到时没回复就追问 |

## 6. 字幕里怎么认

| 字幕里可能看到 | 实际意思 / 标准说法 |
| --- | --- |
| `we can allow with Mia offline` | `we can align with Mia offline`（会后和 Mia 对齐） |
| `I'll see you in the deleting cup meeting` | `I'll see you in the daily sync-up meeting` |
| `discuss it in the post graph` | `discuss it in the post-scrum session` |
| `Your eyes, Bay.` / `Bagde.` | `Bye, guys.` / `Bye.`（道别时的识别噪音，忽略即可） |
| `we think we can end today's sync up meeting` | `I think we can end today's sync-up meeting` |
| `I will follow up this` | `I will follow up on this` |

**读法：** 会议最后两分钟的字幕里，**动词 `will` 和人名**最重要。把每个 `X will …` 都当成一条行动项，抄到自己的笔记里。

## 7. 常见错误

| ❌ 错误 | ✅ 正确 | 原因 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `I will follow up this.` | `I will follow up on this.` | `follow up on + 事`，`follow up with + 人` |
| `Let's discuss it in offline.` | `Let's discuss it offline.` | `offline` 是副词，不加 `in` |
| `I will feedback to you.` | `I'll get back to you.` / `I'll give you feedback.` | `feedback` 是名词 |
| `See you tomorrow's meeting.` | `See you in tomorrow's meeting.` | 要加 `in` |
| `I will send you after meeting.` | `I'll send it to you after the meeting.` | 要有宾语 `it`，`meeting` 前加 `the` |
| `Who will take charge this?` | `Who will take charge of this?` / `Who will take this?` | `take charge of` |
| `Have a nice weekends.` | `Have a nice weekend.` | `a` 后用单数 |

## 8. 你的收尾模板

**被分到任务时：**

| 步骤 | 英文模板 | 中文提示 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 认领 | `Sure, I'll take it.` / `I can take the ________ part.` | 接下任务 |
| 时间 | `I think I can ________ by ________.` | 给出时间 |
| 确认 | `So, just to confirm, I will ________ by ________. Right?` | 复述确认 |

**你主持收尾时：**

| 步骤 | 英文模板 | 中文提示 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 总结 | `So, to summarize, ________.` | 结论 |
| 行动项 | `________ will ________ by ________.` | 谁 + 做什么 + 什么时候 |
| 确认 | `Is that correct? Does that work for everyone?` | 让大家确认 |
| 结束 | `Any other topics? … If not, that's all for today. Thank you, everyone.` | 结束会议 |

填好以后是这样的：

```text
So, to summarize, we will move the theme story to the next sprint. Alex will update the ticket by end of day, and Priya will check the design with the design team by Friday. Is that correct? Okay, any other topics? If not, that's all for today. Thank you, everyone. Have a good day.
总结一下，主题那个 Story 顺延到下个 Sprint。Alex 今天下班前更新工单，Priya 周五前和设计团队确认设计。对吗？好，还有其他话题吗？没有的话，今天就到这里。谢谢大家，祝大家今天顺利。
```

## 10. 练习

**A. 从字幕里找出行动项（谁 + 做什么 + 什么时候）**

> Okay, so yeah, Priya will check with the cloud team and Ken will send the document link today, and for the PR template, Alex, can you take this and share the draft before the next sync?

**B. 改错**

1. I will follow up this issue.
2. Let's discuss it in offline.
3. I will feedback to you tomorrow.
4. See you tomorrow's meeting.

**C. 写一条会后 Teams 消息（3–4 句）**

内容：今天决定先用本地 Runner；Alex 周五前调研云端方案；Ken 确认许可证；有遗漏请告诉我。

<details>
<summary>答案</summary>

**A.**
1. Priya — 和云团队确认（check with the cloud team）— 没说时间（可以追问 `By when?`）
2. Ken — 发文档链接（send the document link）— 今天（today）
3. Alex — 负责 PR 模板，分享草稿（share the draft）— 下次同步会前（before the next sync）

**B.**
1. I will follow up on this issue.
2. Let's discuss it offline.
3. I'll get back to you tomorrow.（或 I'll give you feedback tomorrow.）
4. See you in tomorrow's meeting.

**C.** 参考：

Hi all, here is a quick summary of today's discussion. We will use the local runner for now. Alex will research the cloud runner option by Friday. Ken will check the license status. Please let me know if I missed anything.

</details>
