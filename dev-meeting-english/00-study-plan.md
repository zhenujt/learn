# Study Plan: 16 Weeks, 40 Minutes a Day

> [中文版](00-study-plan.zh.md) · [Course home](README.md) · [Next: How to Read Meeting Subtitles](01-how-to-read-subtitles.md)

## 1. The Idea

You work in China without an English-speaking environment, but you have **real English meetings every week, with subtitles**. That is the best material available:

- The content is your own work, so you can use it immediately.
- The same expressions return every week (`we need to`, `working on`, `that's all for today`), which gives natural spaced repetition.
- Subtitles can be reread as often as you like.

The core of the plan:

> **Build the frame with grammar lessons, practice with your own meeting subtitles, and produce output through your daily stand-up update.**

Three principles:

1. **Store whole sentences, not single words.** Learn `Just to clarify, are you saying that …?`, not just `clarify`.
2. **Fix only one or two problems at a time.**
3. **Use each new expression actively 3–5 times** in updates, Jira comments, and Teams messages.

## 2. The Daily 40 Minutes

| Time | Activity | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10 min | **One grammar point** | Read one or two sections of this week's lesson and read the examples aloud |
| 15 min | **Subtitle reading** | Take 5–8 lines from your latest meeting and apply the [four-step method](01-how-to-read-subtitles.md): topic → main clause → status → action |
| 10 min | **Output** | Write a 3-sentence stand-up update using today's grammar; read it aloud three times; optionally ask an AI tutor to fix only the 1–2 most important problems |
| 5 min | **Review** | Save the 3 most useful sentences on the website's Sentences page; review yesterday's and last week's sentences |

**On busy days:** do only "5 subtitle lines + a 3-sentence update" (15 minutes). Do not break the chain.

**Weekend (one 60-minute session):**

1. Reread a whole meeting's subtitles (30 min). Mark every unclear sentence and analyze three of them.
2. Do one [subtitle drill](practice/01-subtitle-reading-drills.md) (15 min).
3. Write a 5–8 sentence weekly report in English (15 min).

**Listening (twice a week, 5 minutes):** play 30 seconds of a meeting recording **without subtitles**, write the words you catch, then compare with the subtitles.

## 4. Using Subtitles During a Meeting

**Before (2 minutes):** write your 3-sentence update with the [M01](meetings/01-stand-up-updates.md) template.

**During:** do not read every word. Catch four things:

1. **Topic:** `For the web part, …`
2. **Status:** `is done` / `is in progress` / `is blocked`
3. **Who:** the subject of the sentence
4. **Action:** `we need to …` / `can you …` / `I will …`

**After (10 minutes, the most valuable step):** copy 5–8 lines you did not understand; they become your next subtitle-reading material.

## 5. Creating an English Environment in China

- Switch your IDE, Jira, Teams, and phone to English.
- Write Jira comments, commit messages, PR descriptions, and Teams messages in English.
- Read English technical sources: README files, official docs, error messages.
- Use an AI tutor: ask it to fix only the 1–2 problems that most affect understanding, then let it play the meeting host.
- Reuse this week's best sentence at least three times in real work.

## 6. Measuring Progress

Test yourself every four weeks with the same meeting transcript:

| Measure | Method | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Subtitle comprehension | Pick 20 random lines; count lines you can explain | 50% (wk 4) → 70% (wk 8) → 85% (wk 12) → 90% (wk 16) |
| Main-clause detection | Find subject and verb in 10 long sentences | 8/10 from week 8 |
| Output | Speak an update without notes and record it | 1 minute (wk 8), 2 minutes (wk 16) |
| Tests | [Checkpoint tests](practice/02-checkpoint-tests.md) | 70 points to pass |

If you miss a target, do not move on. Spend another week on the lessons where you lost the most points.

## 7. FAQ

**How deep should grammar go?** 🔴 produce it; 🟡 understand and use common forms; 🟢 recognize only; ⚪ skip.

**What about vocabulary?** Do not memorize word lists. Learn words with their sentences from subtitles. Look up meeting words in [Meeting Frequent Words](../meeting-frequent-words.md).

**Pronunciation?** Before speaking, spend one week on stress and linking in [Pronunciation Basics](../zero-to-work-english/01-发音入门/README.md).

**Colleagues use non-standard English. Should I copy it?** Understand it; say the standard form yourself. Lessons show common non-native forms with standard equivalents.
