## Grade 3: People, Things, and Quantities

| Word | Meaning | Subtitle cue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| name | What a person or thing is called | `the file name` names a file |
| this | The thing or idea being pointed to | In `this works`, the previous line may say what `this` refers to |
| look | Direct your eyes; appear to be | `look at the screen` asks you to look; `looks good` states an impression |
| many | A large number of countable things | `how many tickets` asks for a count |
| new | Not there or not known before | `a new issue` could mean a new problem or ticket |

## Grade 4: Times, Places, and People

| Word | Meaning | Subtitle cue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| next | Coming immediately after | `next week` is a time; `what's next?` asks for the next step |
| right | Correct; a request for agreement at the end of a question | `the right branch` means the correct branch |
| before | Earlier than an event or time | `before the demo` sets a time limit |
| ready | Prepared for something | `ready for review` does not mean the review is complete |
| with | Together with, using, or involving someone or something | `check with Alex` means ask Alex to verify something |

## Grade 5: Habits, Plans, and Sequence

| Word | Meaning | Subtitle cue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| usually | Most of the time | `we usually run tests first` describes a habit |
| sometimes | On some occasions | `it sometimes fails` does not mean it always fails |
| first | Before the other steps | `check the logs first` sets the order |
| need | Require something or have to do something | `we need to check` does not say the check is done |
| keep | Retain something or continue doing it | `keep testing` means continue; `keep the old version` means retain it |

## Grade 6: Progress and Status

| Word | Meaning | Subtitle cue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| start | Begin; the point where something begins | `we started testing` does not say testing is complete |
| finish | Complete an action or bring it to an end | `finished testing` does not necessarily mean the tests passed |
| still | Continuing up to the present or the time in question | `it's still running` means it has not stopped |
| change | Make something different; a difference or modification | `one more change` means an additional modification |
| wait | Stay until something happens | `waiting for approval` means it has not arrived yet |

## Grade 7: Asking, Explaining, and Reporting

| Word | Meaning | Subtitle cue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| join | Become part of or take part in | `join the call` means enter the call |
| share | Let others see or use something | `share my screen` means show my screen to others |
| mean | Signify or refer to | `do you mean the test environment?` checks what the speaker was referring to |
| check | Look at something to verify it | `I'll check` is a promise to verify, not a result |
| fix | Repair a problem; a solution to a problem | `we have a fix` does not say it has been deployed |

## Grade 8: Causes, Conditions, and Suggestions

| Word | Meaning | Subtitle cue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| unless | Except if; if not | `unless we get access` introduces an exception |
| issue | A problem or, in a tracker, a work item | `open an issue` means create a work item; `we have an issue` reports a problem |
| suggest | Propose an idea; indicate something indirectly | `I suggest ...` proposes; `the logs suggest ...` points to an inference |
| expect | Think something is likely; consider something necessary or proper | `we expect it by Friday` is not a guarantee |
| depend | Be determined by or require someone or something | `it depends on approval` identifies a prerequisite |

## Grade 9: Conclusions, Risks, and Action Items

| Word | Meaning | Subtitle cue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| confirm | Verify or establish that something is true or settled | `can you confirm the date?` asks someone to check it |
| require | Need or demand | `this requires approval` identifies a requirement |
| affect | Have an influence on | `affect the release` means have an impact on it; compare the noun `effect` |
| resolve | Solve a problem or settle a disagreement | `we resolved the blocker` does not mean the whole task is complete |
| follow up | Take the next action or ask for news afterward | `I'll follow up with the team` means the speaker will contact the team about it |

Start with the five words at each level. Hide the meaning column and try to infer each meaning from the phrase. If you are unsure about several, review that level first. For status words like `ready` and `expect`, ask whether the subtitle states a fact, plan, or condition.
