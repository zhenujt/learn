## 本课能做什么
能说明任务的重要性、截止时间和风险。
## 核心公式
`need to be done by + 时间`；`priority`；`more urgent than`；`risk of + V-ing`。
## 递进例句
1. `This task is urgent.` 2. `It needs to be done by Friday.` 3. `Fixing the payment issue is more urgent because there is a risk of losing orders.`
## 常见错误
`deadline is Friday` 可用，但更明确：`The deadline is Friday.`
## 口语与写作任务
排序三项任务并解释原因；写 80 词优先级更新。
## 自测与答案
“截止日期是周五。”：`The deadline is Friday.`
