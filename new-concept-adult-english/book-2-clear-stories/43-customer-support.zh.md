## 本课能做什么
能礼貌回应简单用户问题，说明下一步。
## 核心公式
`I'm sorry that ...`；`I understand that ...`；`I'll look into ...`；`Could you + 动词?`
## 递进例句
1. `I'm sorry about the problem.` 2. `I understand that you cannot log in.` 3. `I'll look into the issue and update you by tomorrow.`
## 常见错误
`Sorry for you` -> `I'm sorry about the problem.`
## 口语与写作任务
回复一个无法登录的用户；写 80 词支持邮件。
## 自测与答案
“我会调查这个问题。”：`I'll look into this issue.`
