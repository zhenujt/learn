## 本课能做什么
能在正式建议、要求和政策中使用准确结构。
## 核心公式
`suggest/request/require that + 主语 + 动词原形`；`It is essential that + 主语 + 动词原形`。
## 递进例句
1. `I suggest that he wait.` 2. `The policy requires that users change passwords.` 3. `It is essential that the team test the migration.`
## 常见错误
`suggest him to go` -> `suggest that he go` 或 `suggest going`。
## 口语与写作任务
写三条正式建议；写 100 词政策建议。
## 自测与答案
“我建议我们先审查。”：`I suggest that we review it first.`
