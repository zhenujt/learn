## 本课能做什么
能说明已有计划、即时决定和简单预测。

## 核心公式
`be going to + 动词` 表计划；`will + 动词原形` 表即时决定、承诺或预测。`I am going to study tonight.` `I will send it now.`

## 递进例句
1. `I'm going to visit my parents this weekend.` 2. `We will call you after the meeting.` 3. `The team is going to release a small update next week.`

## 常见错误
`I will to call` -> `I will call.` `I going to work` -> `I am going to work.`

## 口语与写作任务
说三个明天计划。写一条项目计划消息，说明下一步和时间。

## 自测与答案
1. `I ___ going to study.` 答案：`am`。2. “我现在就发给你。”：`I will send it to you now.`
