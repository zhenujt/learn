## Goal and Core Formula
Use `Hi + name`, purpose, one clear request, and `Thanks`.
## Examples
`Hi, Sam. Could you send me the link, please?`
## Common Mistakes
`Please send it` may be too direct; use `Could you send it, please?`
## Practice and Answers
Write a 50-word message to confirm a meeting.
