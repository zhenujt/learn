## 本课能做什么
能描述简单故障、请求帮助并说明已尝试的方法。

## 核心公式
`There is/are + 问题`；`I can't + 动词`；`I tried + V-ing/to + 动词`；`Can you + 动词?`

## 递进例句
1. `There is a problem with my phone.` 2. `I can't open the file. Can you help me?` 3. `There is an error in the login page. I tried restarting the app, but it did not help.`

## 常见错误
`Have a problem` 缺主语 -> `I have a problem.` `There have an error` -> `There is an error.`

## 口语与写作任务
说明一个真实的小问题及一次尝试。写 50 词 issue 消息，含现象、已做动作和请求。

## 自测与答案
1. `There ___ two errors.` 答案：`are`。2. “我打不开链接。”：`I can't open the link.`
