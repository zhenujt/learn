# Software Workplace Communication: Captions and Short Replies

[中文版](README.zh.md)

You can begin supported workplace practice before finishing general A1/A2 lessons. The folder's B1 label describes a broad course destination, not an entry requirement or a promise that completion establishes B1. Unfamiliar technical discussions may require more support and language ability.

## Start here

1. Open the [14-unit meeting course](software-meeting-demo-14-day.md) and complete its caption starter: read five short prompts, interpret them, reply, and check the hidden answers.
2. Prioritize clarification, status, blockers, and follow-up in Days 2, 8, 9, and 12. Use a prompt card if needed. Hosting and demos are later or needs-based tasks.
3. Consult one section of the [grammar guide](software-workplace-grammar-guide.md) when a caption is unclear. The optional 12-week grammar schedule is not a second course to finish alongside the site plan.
4. Use Level 1 in the [workplace phrasebook](software-workplace-phrases.md), then add expressions for your actual tasks. Check important owners, dates, conditions, and commitments.

Practise reading, listening, and replies together within the site's daily plan. The 14 units and 24-week plan are flexible references, not proficiency deadlines. Use fictional or explicitly permitted material; do not upload confidential meeting transcripts to external tools.

## Check practical progress

Use an unfamiliar short caption. Can you identify the action, status, time, negation, and condition where present? Can you reply truthfully or ask a specific clarification question? Note whether you needed captions, translation, or a prompt card. Reduce help gradually and repeat patterns that change the meaning when misunderstood.

## Broader topics for later study

Work email, polite requests, introductions, stand-ups, bug reports, requirements, code review, meeting participation, progress updates, cross-time-zone collaboration, technical writing, and interviews remain useful topics. They are not a mandatory sequence before you can join a meeting, and this list does not mean twelve complete standalone lessons are available.
