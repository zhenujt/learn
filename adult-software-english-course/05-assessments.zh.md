# 阶段测评：五次检查是否真正会用

[English](05-assessments.md) · [目录](README.zh.md) · [听力训练](04-listening-lab.zh.md) · [语法帮助](02-grammar-reference.zh.md)

## 怎样公平测

第 4、8、12、16、20 课后分别做测评 1–5。这是课程进度检查，不是标准 CEFR 考试。前期可安排 30–45 分钟，后期 45–60 分钟，也可分次；不是限时速度要求。

第一次不用范文、翻译工具、答案。先看问题，请搭档只读隐藏卡里的英文。可请求重读一句，记录重听和提示，不假装全独立。独自准备 TTS 时若已读到卡片，记为有支持，之后换信息再做陌生测验。没有随附音频。

听读理解允许用中英文简答，分开测“懂不懂”和“写不写得出”。口语写作任务用英文。录音先获同意。独自扮演能检查表达，不能验证面对未知真人的即时互动，有条件后补搭档尝试。

## 共用量规，不设神奇总分

| 维度 | 0：未显示 | 1：起步 | 2：有支持能用 | 3：本任务中能独立用 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 信息 | 关键意思缺失或说反 | 零碎，重要事实需纠正 | 修复后主信息清楚 | 事实、限制、行动清楚 |
| 互动 | 无法回应任务 | 主要背诵，靠提供回复 | 能问答，可停顿重听 | 能处理变化并修复误解 |
| 语言与可懂度 | 听者无法恢复意思 | 形式或发音常遮蔽信息 | 有错误但关键意思可懂 | 句型发音可靠传达本消息 |
| 听读理解 | 主信息未提取 | 部分细节，主要误解 | 有针对性帮助后提取主信息 | 不用看文替代听，能提取重点 |
| 写作用途 | 没有可用消息 | 缺关键信息或组织 | 少量澄清后能行动 | 需要时事实、负责人、时间清楚 |

每项评级旁写一条证据，不把总分换成 CEFR 等级。关键任务能完成、支持量可控、换信息后能纠正误解，就继续。某技能没测记“未测”，不能记通过。

这些无论其他表现如何都优先修复：时间或负责人错、漏否定、编造批准、把未证实根因当事实、漏影响安全的限制。先纠正，再换信息重做薄弱任务，不一定重来整套。口音不同本身不是失败。

## 测评 1：介绍、时间与习惯（第 4 课后）

### 听力

问题：1. 名字职业？2. 平时几点开始？3. 周五几点？4. 电脑在哪？需要时请求拼写。

<details>
<summary>仅给朗读者的卡片 1</summary>

Hi. I'm Eli. E-L-I. I'm a designer. I usually start work at eight thirty. On Friday, I start at nine. This is my desk. My laptop is on the desk, and my charger is in my bag.

</details>

### 阅读

**Noor is a developer. She works at home on Monday. She starts at ten. Her teammate, Alex, works in the office and starts at nine. The team call is on Monday at 11:15 a.m. UTC+0.**

问题：1. Noor 周一在哪工作？2. 谁更早开始？3. 通话何时，含时区？本部分先不提供逐句译文，以检查阅读。

### 口语与写作

认识新同事：问候、姓名职业、问对方姓名和开始时间、说一件自己的物品。搭档卡：你叫 Val，8:45 开始，学习者初听成 8:15；纠正，让学习者确认。再问对方平时在哪工作。个人信息可虚构。

写四至六句介绍和日常，含一个否定和一个所属表达。用学过的句型，不硬翻长句。

### 语言修复

1. I developer. 2. She start at nine. 3. Does you work here? 4. These is my screens.

<details>
<summary>测评 1 答案与示例</summary>

听力：Eli、设计师；平时 8:30；周五 9:00；电脑桌上，充电器包里。阅读：Noor 在家十点开始，Alex 办公室九点更早；周一上午 11:15 UTC+0 通话。Eli 的开始时间未明确上午晚上，不要求答脚本没给的内容。

改错：I am a developer. She starts at nine. Do you work here? These are my screens.

写作：My name is An. I'm a developer. I work at home. I usually start at nine. I don't work on Sunday. This is my laptop. 我叫 An，开发者，在家九点开始，周日不工作，这是我的电脑。

口语证据：对方能听清姓名、角色、所属、正确时间；你能问答而不只介绍。纠正示例：Eight forty-five, not eight fifteen. Is that right? 八点四十五不是十五，对吗？

</details>

补练：be/所属不清回 1–2 课；数字错回发音页和 3 课；习惯提问回 4 课。换名字及 10:15/10:50 重测，不再背 Eli。

## 测评 2：日常需求与当前活动（第 8 课后）

### 听力

问题：1. 哪种饮料没有？2. 顾客改选什么？3. 数量和总价？4. 能用现金吗？

<details>
<summary>仅给朗读者的卡片 2</summary>

Customer: Two coffees, please.

Server: Sorry, we don't have coffee today. We have tea and water.

Customer: Two teas, then. How much are they?

Server: Six dollars in total.

Customer: Can I pay in cash?

Server: Yes, cash is fine.

</details>

### 阅读

**Jules usually works in Room 10. Today Jules is using Room 18 because Room 10 is closed. Room 18 is next to the stairs and opposite the kitchen. Jules is checking a report now and is not available until 2 p.m. local time.**

问题：1. 平时和今天房间？2. 两个位置线索？3. 正在做什么？4. 现在有空吗？另一个国家的人知道这个时间够精确吗？

### 口语与写作

你找 18 室并求助。搭档给你漏听的一条：Turn right after the stairs（楼梯后右转）。请重复、复述，再问现在是否有空。搭档说在查报告，三点能帮。确认时间和谁的当地时间。

写五至七句消息：平时地点、今天地点、路线、当前动作、稍后通话请求。包含约定时区或明确询问时区。另外口头点两个餐品，遇一种缺货时回应。

### 语言修复

1. I am usually work here. 2. She checking the report now. 3. There is two rooms. 4. Could you to repeat that?

<details>
<summary>测评 2 答案与示例</summary>

听力：咖啡没有，改两杯茶，总价六美元，可现金。点单 two teas 是 two cups of tea 的自然简说。阅读：平时 10、今天因关闭换 18；楼梯旁、厨房对面；查报告；现在没空，当地下午两点后可用，跨时区需确认。

改错：I usually work here. She is checking the report now. There are two rooms. Could you repeat that?

写作：I usually work in Room 10. Today I'm in Room 18. It is next to the stairs. Turn right after the stairs. I'm checking a report now. Could we talk at three? Do you mean your local time?

口语证据：会追问漏听方向，保留口语任务新约三点，不沿用阅读两点。点餐：I'd like rice and water, please. 缺米饭则 A sandwich instead, please. How much is it? 改三明治并问价。

</details>

补练：澄清笼统回 5 课；数量点餐回 6 课；方位回 7 课；习惯与当前混淆回 8 课。换房间、方向、动作再测。

## 测评 3：过去、完成与选择（第 12 课后）

### 听力

问题：1. 昨天做完什么？2. 什么没完成？3. 哪个依赖影响估时？4. 周二保证完成吗？5. 中午承诺什么？

<details>
<summary>仅给朗读者的卡片 3</summary>

Yesterday I updated the settings and sent the notes to Ren. I've finished the notes, but I haven't finished testing. I need a new test account. If I get it on Monday morning, I expect to finish by Tuesday. I'll confirm the estimate at noon on Monday, UTC plus zero, even if I still don't have access.

</details>

### 阅读

**We need a simple report by Wednesday. Option A takes one day and includes a table. Option B takes four days and includes charts as well. We can start on Monday. Charts are useful but not required for this task. Both options need approved data. Approval is still pending.**

问题：1. 假设开始时权限就绪，哪个更合期限？2. B 多什么？3. 哪件事可能阻碍两方案？4. 能如实说工作已获批准吗？

### 口语与写作

说 60–90 秒：昨天写测试，今天跑，最终结果未出。搭档问何时完成，你请求缺失权限并给条件估计。搭档又说权限晚两天，修改估计或承诺重新计算，不能仍把旧日期说死。

写 60–90 词从阅读中选报告方案，带原因、限制、依赖、下次更新。字数指导范围，不是通过门槛。

### 语言修复

1. Did you sent it? 2. I have finished it yesterday. 3. If access will arrive, we can start. 4. Option A is more faster.

<details>
<summary>测评 3 答案与示例</summary>

听力：昨天更新设置、发记录给 Ren；记录已完但测试未完；需周一上午新账号；周二是有条件估计；周一 UTC+0 中午确认估时，不是完成测试。阅读：A 一天更适合周三前，B 四天多图表；两者都需数据批准，仍待批准。

改错：Did you send it? I finished it yesterday. If access arrives, we can start. Option A is faster.

写作：I recommend option A because we need the report by Wednesday. It takes one day, so it fits the schedule better than option B if we can start on Monday. It provides a table but no charts. Charts are not required for this task. Both options depend on approved data, and approval is still pending. I'll check the approval status and send an update on Monday morning.

意思：推荐一天含表格的 A，周一开始则更合时间，不含非必须图表；依赖未批准数据，周一上午查后更新。

口语证据：三个时间状态不同，权限延迟后有实质修订。That changes the plan. I'll check the remaining work and revise the estimate after we confirm access. 计划变了，查剩余工作，确认权限后修改估时。

</details>

补练：顺序回 9 课；估计承诺回 10 课；完成进行回 11 课；比较条件回 12 课。换依赖和方案取舍再测。

## 测评 4：缺陷、需求与评审（第 16 课后）

### 听力

问题：1. 格式和数量？2. 上限是否含边界？3. 超限怎么处理？4. 什么仍未决定？

<details>
<summary>仅给朗读者的卡片 4</summary>

For this version, users can upload one CSV file at a time. The maximum size is five megabytes, and a file exactly at the limit is allowed. A larger file must show an error and must not be uploaded. Multiple files are excluded. We haven't decided whether an empty file should be accepted. Please confirm that with the requester before implementing the empty-file case.

</details>

### 阅读

**Bug UP-9: test environment, Browser S 4. Select one CSV file above the agreed size limit and click Upload. Expected: error; upload blocked. Actual: upload begins and no error appears. Observed in both of two attempts. Other browsers not checked. Review: correcting the size check and adding an above-limit test are required. Renaming the helper is optional.**

问题：1. 复现步骤？2. 违反什么要求？3. 频率与未测范围？4. 改名必须吗？已批准吗？

### 口语与写作

向搭档解释缺陷，对方问浏览器、频率、未知根因。不能称所有用户都受影响。再澄清空文件：需求方没决定，指定后续确认，不编造接受规则。与评审者讨论必须修复，提出保留原错误提示的担忧，约定测试。

写 70–110 词缺陷报告或评审回复：环境、观察、必需下一步、未知范围、请求，不整段照抄阅读。

### 语言修复

1. It happens when I clicks Upload. 2. Could you clarify what should we do? 3. I suggest to add a test. 4. Users don't have to upload large files. 原意是禁止上传超限文件。

<details>
<summary>测评 4 答案与示例</summary>

听力：一次一个 CSV，上限含 5 MB，超限报错且不上传，多文件排除，空文件未决。字节定义重要时继续问，脚本未给。阅读：选择超限 CSV 点上传；违反大小限制和阻止上传；两次均发生、其他浏览器未测；改名可选、未提供批准。

改错：It happens when I click Upload. Could you clarify what we should do? I suggest adding a test. Users must not upload files above the limit. 系统必须执行已约定限制。

示例：In the test environment on Browser S 4, an above-limit CSV begins uploading without an error. I reproduced this in both of two attempts by selecting the file and clicking Upload. The agreed behavior is to reject it and show an error. Other browsers have not been checked, and the cause is not confirmed. I'll correct the size check and add the required test while preserving the current error wording. Could the requester also confirm whether empty files are allowed? I'll leave the optional rename unchanged.

意思：描述证据、预期、未测范围和未知根因，修改检查加测试保留提示，请确认空文件，暂不改名。

口语证据：事实、未知、必须项、真正澄清问题都明确。猜得合理的根因也不能当已证实事实。

</details>

补练：进度请求回 13 课；复现证据回 14 课；边界可选回 15 课；反馈回应回 16 课。换文件类型和缺字段问题再测。

## 测评 5：新的综合流程（第 20 课后）

### 阅读：新项目

**Feature: invitation links for a fictional team workspace. A user with the admin role can create one link valid for 24 hours. Ordinary members cannot create links. Expired links must show an error and must not add a member. Bulk invitations are out of scope. The supported browser list and how to display the expiration time are still open. Niko reviews the requirements. Sara owns test accounts. Monday is a target, not an approved release date.**

辅助词：admin 管理员；invitation 邀请；expired 过期的；bulk 批量。问题：1. 谁能创建？2. 过期链接必须做什么、不能做什么？3. 两个开放问题？4. 周一批准了吗？这是语言任务背景，不是完整安全设计。

### 听力：项目更新

问题：1. 哪项通过？2. 什么失败？3. 什么仍受阻？4. 各时间谁做什么？5. 发布获批了吗？

<details>
<summary>仅给朗读者的卡片 5</summary>

The valid-link test passed in Browser T version six. The expired-link test failed twice: it showed an error, but it still added the member. We haven't checked the ordinary-member permission case because the test account is missing. Sara will provide that account tomorrow at nine, UTC plus zero. Niko will review the evidence at eleven tomorrow, in the same time zone. Monday remains a target. We must not describe the expired-link behavior as correct just because an error appeared.

</details>

### 口语：会议、演示与变化

只用五个关键词准备，不读全文。纸上演示功能一分钟，再讨论失败和缺失测试两分钟。问一个需求问题，请求账号。搭档问 Can we release on Monday?、Do all browsers fail?、What does an expired link do now?（周一能发布吗、所有浏览器都失败吗、过期链接现在做什么）。

第一轮后，搭档给新信息：Sara says the account will not be ready until tomorrow at noon, UTC+0. Niko's eleven o'clock review is still scheduled. 账号延到明天中午，十一点评审仍安排着。说明冲突，提议改评审或明确做部分评审，请确认。不能擅改别人的会议，也不能称缺失测试十一点前能测完。

### 写作：交接与问题更新

写 100–150 词交接：目的、范围、证据、缺测项、新依赖、提议下一步、发布状态。另加三句事实问题更新。没有生产影响或根因证据，涉及就标未知，不宣布恢复。

### 语言修复

1. The test was pass yesterday. 2. The link shows an error, so no member was added. 与证据矛盾。3. Could you confirm when will the account be ready? 4. We have approved the release. 没有批准。

<details>
<summary>测评 5 答案与交接示例</summary>

阅读：管理员能创建 24 小时有效链接；普通成员不能创建；过期报错且不能加人；浏览器范围与到期时间显示未决；周一未批准。听力：T 6 有效链接通过；过期链接两次报错却仍加人；普通成员权限缺账号未测；原 Sara 明天 UTC+0 09:00 给，Niko 明天 11:00 查证据；后续变更只把账号改中午，不自动改会议。

改错：The test passed yesterday. 测试结果通常用主动；其他语境可有被动 The test was passed yesterday。The link showed an error, but it still added the member. 报错但仍加人。Could you confirm when the account will be ready? The release has not been approved.

交接：This feature lets admins create one invitation link valid for 24 hours. Ordinary members must not create links, and bulk invitations are out of scope. The valid-link test passed in Browser T version six. However, the expired-link test failed twice: it showed an error but still added the member. The ordinary-member permission case is untested because the account is missing. Sara now expects to provide it tomorrow at noon, UTC+0, after Niko's scheduled eleven o'clock review. Could we move the full evidence review until after that test, or use eleven o'clock for a partial review? Please confirm the arrangement. Browser scope and expiration-time display still need clarification. Monday remains a target, not an approved release date.

意思：范围和证据明确，指出账号晚于评审，提议改完整评审或先部分评审，请确认；开放需求未定、周一未批准。

问题更新：The expired-link test added a member in both attempts despite showing an error. The cause and production impact have not been confirmed. We need to verify the correction and the missing permission case before requesting a release decision. 两次报错仍加人，根因生产影响未知，需验证修复与权限测试再请求决策。

口语证据：对方懂用途与实际失败；你如实答未测范围，识别冲突，提议而不是假称已确认新安排。

</details>

补练：准确纪要回 17 课；解释功能回 18 课；不确定事故状态回 19 课；连接任务回 20 课。换新功能、声音、至少一个未知变更再测。

## 记录与下一步

每次一行：日期、测评号、任务、事实是否对、用了什么支持、怎样修复、一个改进目标、换题重测日期。录音和文字仅在隐私允许时保留。照抄范文不能记为独立产出。

第 5 次后选实际最弱项：漏信息就多短篇不看文听；互动弱就真人问答；解码慢就分级短阅读；请求不清就重写消息。日常英语与工作场景继续并行。需要外部等级估计请合格教师或合适标准化考试，本课程不能自行认证等级。
