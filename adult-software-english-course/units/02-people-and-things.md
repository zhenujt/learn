# Unit 2: People and Things

[中文](02-people-and-things.zh.md) · [Course](../README.md) · [Next](03-numbers-and-time.md)

## Goal and words

After Unit 1, identify objects and describe states. Words: laptop, screen, charger (supplies power), desk, ready (prepared), new, old, this (nearby singular), these (nearby plural).

## Dialogue and reading

A: Is this your laptop?
B: Yes, it is. It's new.
A: Where is the charger?
B: It's on the desk.
A: Are these your screens?
B: No, they aren't. They're Lee's screens.

Read: **Lee is a designer. Her desk is near the door. Her laptop is old, but it is ready to use.** An old object can still work; `but` contrasts two ideas.

## Pattern

Match `be`: I **am**; you/we/they **are**; he/she/it **is**. Put `not` after it to make a negative: `It is not ready.` Move it before the person or thing to ask: `Is it ready?`

Use `a laptop`, `an old laptop`, and `two laptops`. `a/an` depends on the next sound: `an old ...`, but `a new ...`. After identifying an object, `the laptop` can refer to that known object. `my/your/her` already identify ownership: say `my laptop`, not `a my laptop`.

`This is ...` is singular; `These are ...` is plural. `Lee's` shows ownership. Adjectives do not gain plural `s`: `new screens`, not `news screens`.

## Practice

1. Fill: She ___ a designer. They ___ ready.
2. Change to a question: The laptop is ready.
3. Correct: These is my screen.
4. Choose: a / an old charger.
5. Reading: whose desk is near the door? Is her laptop unusable?

## Listen, speak, and write

Listen to the dialogue without text and identify which objects belong to speaker B and which to Lee. Then listen to a version with `not new` and explain the changed fact.

Point to three safe household objects. Ask and answer about ownership and state. Your partner should deliberately name one wrong owner; correct politely. Write four sentences about objects, including one negative and one plural.
