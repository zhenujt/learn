# Unit 10: Plans and Commitments

[中文](10-plans.zh.md) · [Course](../README.md) · [Next](11-completion.md)

## Goal and words

Discuss future work and limits. Words: plan, tomorrow, deadline (latest agreed completion time), available, confirm, probably, before, by.

## Dialogue and reading

A: What are you going to do tomorrow?
B: I'm going to test the new page.
A: Can you finish by Thursday?
B: I need to check the remaining tests first.
A: When can you confirm?
B: I'll confirm the estimate this afternoon.
A: Thanks. Please tell me if the plan changes.

Read: **Testing plan: start Wednesday; estimated finish Thursday; review with Sam on Friday at 10 a.m. UTC+0. Estimate depends on access to the test environment.** An estimate is not an unconditional guarantee.

## Pattern

`am/is/are going to + base verb` often expresses an intention or plan. `will + base verb` can express a decision, offer, prediction, or promise. `I'm meeting Sam on Friday` uses present continuous for an arrangement. These forms can overlap; do not invent a strict rule that one always means certainty.

`can` expresses ability or possibility: `I can check`. `can't` negates it; confirm negatives if unclear. `by Thursday` means no later than Thursday; `before Thursday` excludes Thursday itself; `on Thursday` locates the event on that day. Clarify an exact time when necessary.

## Practice

1. Fill: She is going ___ test. I will ___ (send) an update.
2. Which means a deadline: by Friday / on Friday?
3. Answer a deadline request when you do not know yet.
4. Reading: is Thursday guaranteed? When is the review?
5. Correct: I will to check it.

## Four-skill task

Listen to the dialogue. What is promised this afternoon: finishing the test or confirming the estimate? Write it before checking the script.

Plan a fictional task with a partner who asks for an earlier deadline. Explain what you know and what you must check. Write a short plan with start, estimate, dependency, and next update. Review Unit 3 time zones.
