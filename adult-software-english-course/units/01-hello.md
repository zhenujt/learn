# Unit 1: Hello and Names

[中文](01-hello.zh.md) · [Course](../README.md) · [Next](02-people-and-things.md)

## Goal and words

Introduce yourself and ask a name. No prerequisite. Words: hello (greeting), name (what someone is called), my (belonging to me), your (belonging to you), developer (someone who builds software), team (people working together).

## Dialogue

A: Hello. I'm Mina.
B: Hi, Mina. My name is Ben.
A: Nice to meet you, Ben.
B: Nice to meet you, too.
A: I'm a developer. Are you a developer?
B: Yes, I am. Welcome to the team.

Read: **Mina is a developer. Ben is a developer, too.** Both people have the same job. `too` means also.

## Understand the pattern

`I am Mina` identifies me. `I'm` is the short form of `I am`; the meaning is the same. `My name is Ben` gives a name. `I am a developer` gives one person's job; keep `a` before the singular job noun.

Ask `What is your name?` or `What's your name?` Use `Are you ...?` to ask about the other person. Answer `Yes, I am` or `No, I'm not`. Do not use `Yes, I'm` alone as this short answer.

Say `Nice to MEET you` in a comfortable short group. Do not copy the sounds through Chinese character spellings; use a spoken model.

## Practice

1. Complete: I ___ Ben. My name ___ Ben.
2. Put in order: name / your / is / What / ?
3. Reply truthfully: Are you a developer?
4. Reading: who is new to the team? What job do both people have?
5. Repair: I developer.

## Listen, speak, and write

Ask a partner or speech reader to read the dialogue while you hide it. Write the two names and jobs; replay with text. Swap Mina and Ben for other names and listen again. The goal is identifying information, not memorizing an accent.

Role-play meeting a colleague: greet, give a name, ask a name, state a job, and close politely. First use the dialogue, then a card with only those five actions. Write three sentences introducing yourself. Use invented personal details if preferred.

## Check and review

Can your listener identify your name and job? Can you ask rather than only recite? Tomorrow, introduce a different person using their name: `Ben is a developer.` Review this after one, three, and seven days.
