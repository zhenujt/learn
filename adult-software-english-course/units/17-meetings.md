# Unit 17: Participating in Meetings

[中文](17-meetings.zh.md) · [Course](../README.md) · [Next](18-demos.md)

## Goal and vocabulary

Join a discussion, check understanding, and record decisions. Words: agenda (topics for discussion), interrupt (speak before someone finishes), decision (agreed choice), action item (assigned next step), owner, due (expected by a time), summarize, postpone (move to later), unresolved (not decided).

## Dialogue and reading

A: Today's question is whether we can release on Friday.
B: Sorry to interrupt. Is the large-file test complete?
A: Not yet. Dana is running it this afternoon.
C: Then let's decide after we have the result.
B: So Friday is a target, not a confirmed release date. Is that correct?
A: Correct. Dana will post the result by four today, UTC+0.
C: I'll arrange a decision call for tomorrow at ten, UTC+0.
B: I'll note those actions. Who will make the release decision?
A: Priya, the release owner, will decide on that call.

Read: **Agenda: release readiness. Evidence: small files passed; large files pending. Decision: postpone the release decision until tomorrow's call. Actions: Dana posts results today by 16:00 UTC+0; Chris arranges tomorrow's 10:00 UTC+0 call; Priya decides release readiness. Friday remains a target.** Deciding to wait is not deciding to release.

## Pattern

Enter briefly: `Could I add one point?` Clarify: `Do you mean ...?` Repair: `I missed the last point. Could you repeat it?` Close a topic: `Can we confirm the owner and next step?` You do not need to comment on every turn.

An indirect question uses statement order: `Could you confirm who owns this task?` Compare the direct `Who owns this task?` In this subject question, `who` is already the subject, so do is unnecessary.

Report with an explicit source: `Dana said the test was still running.` In a same-day summary, `Dana says the test is still running` can emphasize a current report. Preserve time references: replace ambiguous `tomorrow` with the actual date in durable notes after checking it. Do not invent a date for this fictional dialogue.

## Practice

1. Repair: Could you confirm when is the call?
2. Write a brief polite interruption to ask about testing.
3. Reading: what was decided, and what remains undecided?
4. List the three owners and their actions.
5. Correct the misleading summary: We agreed to release on Friday.

## Listen, speak, and write

Listen without text and fill four columns: topic, decision, action/owner, time. Ask a reader to change one owner and one time in a second reading. Compare notes rather than relying on memory of the first version.

Three roles: developer has a pending test; coordinator wants Friday; release owner requires a result. In a three-minute discussion, each person must ask or answer a question. Solo practice can cover all roles, but use a partner later for unfamiliar responses. Finish with a read-back and an opportunity to correct it.

Write 70-100 words of meeting notes. Separate confirmed decisions from targets and open questions. Avoid a transcript of every sentence.
