# Unit 13: Tasks and Stand-ups

[中文](13-tasks-and-standups.zh.md) · [Course](../README.md) · [Next](14-bugs.md)

## Goal and vocabulary

Give a short update that helps a team act. Prerequisite: Units 9-12. A stand-up is a short team coordination meeting; you do not necessarily stand. Words: task (piece of work), progress (movement toward completion), blocker (something preventing progress), access (permission to use), owner (responsible person), update, remaining (not finished), dependency (something needed first).

## Dialogue and reading

A: What's your update on the export task?
B: I've finished the page. I'm testing the download now.
A: Any blockers?
B: I can't test large files because I don't have access to the test data.
A: Who can help?
B: Could Dana give me access? I'll share the results after testing.
A: I'll ask Dana after this meeting.
B: Thanks. I'll update the ticket by three today, even if access is still missing.

Read: **Ticket EX-12: Export page complete. Small-file download passed. Large-file test pending. Dana owns test-data access. Next status update: today, 15:00 UTC+0. Release date not confirmed.** `pending` means waiting or not complete, not failed. A ticket is a work-tracking record, not a travel ticket here.

## Pattern and listening focus

Build an update from facts: `I've finished ...` + `I'm working on ...` + `I'm blocked by ...` + a specific request or next update. You need not always recite all four when some are irrelevant.

`I need access to the data` requests a resource; `I need Dana to check it` requests an action by a person. In `I'll update the ticket after I test it`, the time clause uses present `test`, not `will test`. Be explicit about what `it` refers to when several objects are mentioned.

Listen especially for `can't`, `not yet`, and the action after `could`. Reporting progress does not make an unfinished test complete.

## Practice

1. Fill: I need access ___ the environment. I need Jo ___ review it.
2. Repair: I'm finish the page yesterday.
3. Turn "I am blocked" into a specific blocker and request using the reading.
4. Reading: did the large-file test fail? Is the release date confirmed?
5. What is promised by three in the dialogue? What is not promised?

## Listen, speak, and write

Hide the dialogue. Ask a reader to speak it; note done/current/blocker/request/update. Replay only the part you missed. Then have the reader change the missing resource from data to an account and give a fresh summary.

Role cards: A has completed a page but lacks a test account. B can provide the account tomorrow, not today. Negotiate what A can do meanwhile and when to update. Change roles with a missing design decision. Give a 30-60 second update from five keywords, then answer two unprepared questions.

Write a 50-80 word ticket comment including evidence, remaining work, help needed, and next update. Do not invent a completion date you cannot support.
