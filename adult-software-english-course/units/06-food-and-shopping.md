# Unit 6: Food and Shopping

[中文](06-food-and-shopping.zh.md) · [Course](../README.md) · [Next](07-places-and-directions.md)

## Goal and words

Order a simple meal and resolve a mismatch. Words: water, rice, sandwich, coffee, small, large, price, without, bill (amount to pay).

## Dialogue and reading

A: What would you like?
B: I'd like a sandwich and some water, please.
A: A small or a large bottle?
B: A small bottle. Does the sandwich have cheese?
A: Yes, it does.
B: Could I have it without cheese?
A: Yes. That's eight dollars in total.
B: Thank you. Can I pay by card?

Read: **Menu: sandwich $6; small water $2; coffee $3. Card and cash accepted.** A sandwich and water cost $8 before any extra charges not stated here.

## Pattern

`I'd like ...` means `I would like ...`, a polite way to order. Countable nouns have units: `a sandwich`, `two sandwiches`. Water and rice are usually uncountable: `some water`, `some rice`, not `a water` in basic usage. Use `a bottle of water` when counting containers.

`How much is it?` asks price; `How many sandwiches?` asks count. `some` commonly appears in offers or requests as well as statements. `any` often appears in questions/negatives: `Do you have any rice?` `We don't have any rice.` These are tendencies, not absolute rules.

Pronounce the important item and quantity clearly; politeness does not require a long sentence.

## Practice

1. Choose: a / some rice; two sandwich / sandwiches.
2. Ask the price of one coffee.
3. Order one sandwich without cheese.
4. Reading: what is the price of a sandwich and coffee? Is card payment allowed?
5. You receive coffee but ordered water. Say what is wrong politely.

## Listen, speak, and write

Listen to the dialogue and record food, drink, modification, and price. Then request a version with a large bottle and listen for the size, not an assumed new price.

Role-play customer and server. Server: one menu item is unavailable; say so. Customer: choose an alternative and confirm price. Write a three-line order for two people with quantities and one special request.
