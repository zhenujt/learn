# Unit 3: Numbers and Time

[中文](03-numbers-and-time.zh.md) · [Course](../README.md) · [Next](04-daily-routines.md)

## Goal and words

Exchange a time and confirm details. Words: meeting (scheduled discussion), today, tomorrow, morning, afternoon, number, at, on. Learn number forms using the [sound reference](../01-sounds-and-spelling.md).

## Dialogue and reading

A: What time is the meeting?
B: At three fifteen on Tuesday.
A: Three fifty?
B: No, three fifteen: one-five, not five-zero.
A: Is that your local time?
B: Yes. I'll send a calendar invitation.

Read: **Team check-in: Tuesday, 15 September, 3:15 p.m., UTC+0. Room 204.** This written notice supplies a date, time zone, and location. Do not assume an unstated time zone in a real meeting.

## Pattern

Use `at 3:15`, `on Tuesday`, `in September`, but simply `tomorrow`. Ask `What time is ...?` For prices use `How much is ...?` `three fifteen` means 3:15; `three fifty` means 3:50. Say digits separately for room or phone numbers when helpful.

`I'll send ...` means `I will send ...`; learn this short promise as a phrase now and study future patterns in Unit 10. Stress the difference in `fifTEEN` and `FIFty`, but confirm through digits if sound alone is unclear.

## Practice

1. Fill: ___ Monday; ___ 9:30; ___ October.
2. Write 8:45 in words.
3. Ask for the time of a call.
4. Read the notice: what room and time zone? Does 3:15 p.m. mean early morning?
5. Your listener says 3:50 when you mean 3:15. Correct and confirm.

## Listen, speak, and write

Have someone read 13, 30, 15, 50, 18, 80 in a different order. Write what you hear and compare, then repeat with times. Use ordinary comfortable speed, not an artificial pronunciation test alone.

Arrange a fictional ten-minute call. Both people must confirm day, time, and zone. Write a two-sentence invitation including all three. Do not share real phone numbers in practice.
