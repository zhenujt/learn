# Unit 5: Questions and Clarification

[中文](05-questions-and-clarification.zh.md) · [Course](../README.md) · [Next](06-food-and-shopping.md)

## Goal and words

Prerequisite: Units 1-4. Keep a conversation going when information is missing. Words: repeat (say again), slowly, mean, spell, word, understand, question, sorry.

## Dialogue and reading

A: Please open the shared folder.
B: Sorry, what does "shared" mean?
A: It means other people can use it, too.
B: Which folder do you mean?
A: The design folder.
B: Could you spell "design"?
A: D-E-S-I-G-N.
B: Thank you. I understand now.

Read: **Please use the shared folder, not your private folder. Ask Jo if you need access.** The contrast between shared and private matters; understanding `folder` alone is insufficient.

## Pattern

`what` asks about a thing or meaning; `who` a person; `where` a place; `when` a time; `why` a reason; `how` a method; `which` a choice. Compare `Where is the folder?` (be question) and `Where do you work?` (ordinary verb question).

`Could you + base verb ...?` makes a polite request: `Could you repeat that?` Do not add `to`: not `Could you to repeat?` Learn `What does ... mean?` as a useful pattern; `mean` stays in the base form after does.

If you heard the sound but not the meaning, ask for an explanation. If you missed the sound, ask for repetition. You do not have to say "yes" to end confusion.

## Practice

1. Choose a question word: ___ is the meeting? In Room 2.
2. Correct: What does this means?
3. Request a slower repetition politely.
4. Reading: which folder is required? Who can help with access?
5. You know what folder means but not which folder. Ask precisely.

## Listen, speak, and write

Have a partner read the dialogue without showing it. Identify the folder and spell its name. Ask the partner to change it to `test folder`; confirm the new name.

Information gap: A has an invented room name, time, and folder; B has none. B must obtain all three using questions. Switch roles with different details. Write a two-sentence clarification message, not just "I don't understand."
